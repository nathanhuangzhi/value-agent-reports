The external backdrop remains stable, but the market’s immediate focus is whether the mix‑shift can offset top‑line pressure.

The strategy emphasizes capital efficiency and a pivot toward services, positioning the company as a healthcare platform rather than a pure rental operator. However, credibility is under scrutiny: repeated analyst price‑target cuts and a vocal bear thesis on demand create a high‑stakes reality check around the guidance trajectory. Early results showed margin progression but did not fully resolve the growth gap, leaving execution risk and market confidence delicately balanced heading into the remainder of the year.
