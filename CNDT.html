INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-23

Business & market dynamics: Conduent’s top line remains under pressure, but the revenue decline is proving less severe than market whisper numbers had feared. The portfolio is mixed: government and transportation segments are posting growth, while the commercial business continues to contract meaningfully. Encouragingly, new business signings have increased for six consecutive quarters, pointing to gradually improving demand momentum and early signs of revenue base stabilization.

Management strategy and execution: New CEO Harsha Agadi is driving an aggressive turnaround centered on cost discipline and margin uplift, with early execution delivering markedly improved profitability and cash flow. The divestiture of the transit unit to Modaxo underscores a deliberate portfolio-sharpening strategy, shedding non-core assets to focus on higher-return areas. Market reaction has been favorable, suggesting growing confidence that management’s actions are credible and that the early-stage transformation is gaining traction.
