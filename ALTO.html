The company significantly outpaced both the broader market and key industry peers over the past year, reflecting a strong competitive recovery narrative as the sector moves from a period of persistent losses to steady profitability.

This disciplined repositioning, combined with strategic use of tax credit income and ample liquidity to fund capital projects, has restored credibility. The market has responded with a series of analyst upgrades and price-target increases, rewarding management’s improved guidance posture and capital allocation discipline as the company delivers on its promise of a more durable and profitable trajectory.
