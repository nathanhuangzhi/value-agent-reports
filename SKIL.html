INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-06

Business & market dynamics: Skillsoft’s core Talent Development Solutions segment is gaining real traction in AI-native skills management, with the new Percipio platform winning early customers and AI-tool usage accelerating among adopters. However, the divestiture of the legacy Global Knowledge business is shrinking total revenue, creating a stark disconnect: robust underlying segment profitability sits alongside deep market skepticism that has left the stock at an extreme discount.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership completed the Global Knowledge sale, sharpening focus on a pure-play AI-driven platform. The Percipio launch and delivery of TDS segment profitability and free cash flow above guidance signal credible early execution. The strategic narrative now hinges on integrating Codecademy and proving the AI-native model can scale margins and monetization; for now, the market remains guarded despite the operational progress.
