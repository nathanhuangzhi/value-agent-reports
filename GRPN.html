INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-11

Business & market dynamics: Groupon’s refreshed mission—getting people offline through value-driven local experiences—confronted a mixed reality to start 2026. North America Local remained under pressure, hampered by marketing channel headwinds, weather disruptions, and softer transaction volumes, even as the active customer base expanded. International Local offered a brighter spot, with particular vitality once Giftcloud distortions are stripped out, signaling that demand for curated local offers endures outside the core market. Overall, the top-line was sluggish and engagement per user slipped, reflecting a business still struggling to reignite consistent demand in its home region despite pockets of international strength.

Management strategy and execution: CEO Dušan Šenkypl openly acknowledged first-quarter results fell short of expectations, framing the miss as a catalyst to accelerate the company’s rebuild around artificial intelligence. The centerpiece is “Project Foundry,” an effort to make Groupon an AI-native organization through pilots like AI voice agents for merchant outreach and broad automation of marketing and product functions. This technological pivot is paired with renewed shareholder pressure: activist investor Nick Nemeth is publicly urging a platform overhaul, a rebranding aimed at the thrifting generation, and more aggressive buybacks. Management’s credibility now hinges on whether this AI-powered transformation can reverse the sluggish North American momentum and satisfy calls for bolder capital returns.
