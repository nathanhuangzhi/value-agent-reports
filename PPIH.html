The board completed a strategic review and affirmed the independent growth plan, signaling confidence in the current path. Credibility is reinforced by rising institutional interest, including a new stake from 683 Capital, and the company’s recent inclusion in the Russell 2000 and 3000 indexes, reflecting improved market visibility.
