INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-12

Business & market dynamics: International cannabis demand is surging, with record export sales and capacity ramps in Europe that reinforce a competitive EU GMP advantage. The Canadian cannabis business remains steady, while the legacy greenhouse produce operation adds a consumer-staples buffer. Growth is clouded by persistent U.S. regulatory uncertainty and intensifying global competition, creating a divergence between strong operational momentum and a stock under pressure.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has restructured to prioritize M&A, installing the CFO to spearhead deal-making while retaining his operational role. The company raised fresh equity and is pushing ahead with greenhouse facility expansions in the Netherlands and beyond. The pivot signals ambition, but the market attaches high execution risk to the strategy, with credibility resting on management’s ability to close accretive transactions and convert capacity investments into durable returns.
