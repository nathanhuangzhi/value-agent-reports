INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Apyx’s surgical aesthetics franchise—anchored by Renuvion and the newer AYON Body Contouring System—is riding strong procedure demand. A secular tailwind is forming as GLP-1-driven weight loss expands the pool of patients seeking skin-tightening and body contouring. International expansion is gaining early traction, and the product cycle looks healthy. While top-line momentum is evident, the company remains unprofitable as it invests to scale its commercial presence.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership delivered a beat-and-raise quarter, lifting full-year revenue guidance and reinforcing market confidence in the commercial trajectory. The AYON launch is progressing, international channels are being built, and cost-control efforts have narrowed losses. Analysts are turning more constructive, citing operational discipline and improved guidance credibility. The narrative is shifting toward a growth platform with secular exposure, though sustained profitability remains the key milestone to watch.
