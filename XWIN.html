Additional speculative momentum comes from an indirect investment tied to SpaceX’s expected IPO, which frames the company as having multiple, largely uncorrelated growth drivers. The overall tone in available commentary is optimistic, highlighting accelerating diversification and emerging recurring revenue streams, though the AI business remains in its early stage.

Market-facing materials, including an initiated BUY coverage, reflect a bullish reception to these decisions, but it is worth noting that much of the available information originates from company-solicited or promotional outlets, which may color the perceived execution narrative.
