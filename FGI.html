Tariff uncertainty remains a live risk, though the company is actively working to mitigate the impact through supply‑chain diversification.

Management strategy and execution: The Brands, Products, and Channels (BPC) strategy is starting to show results, underpinned by new customer wins, dealer network expansion, and a push into higher‑growth segments. Leadership reaffirmed full‑year 2026 guidance, signaling conviction in the strategic plan even after a quarterly loss, and is proactively managing tariff headwinds. The market has rewarded the execution narrative, reflecting improved sentiment that the company can sustain momentum through macro turbulence.
