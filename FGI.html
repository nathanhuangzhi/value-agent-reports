Gross margin has held steady, signaling resilient pricing or cost management despite the revenue softness. The market appears to be focusing on these stabilizing signals and international growth potential, driving a sharp stock rally even as top-line contraction persists.

Early momentum from new initiatives, along with proactive tariff-mitigation efforts, has bolstered market confidence. While the business remains unprofitable, management’s tone is optimistic and its execution on margins and cost control is being rewarded, supporting a narrative of gradual operational improvement amid an uncertain backdrop.
