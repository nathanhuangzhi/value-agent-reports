Business & market dynamics: Core floral demand contracted sharply while gourmet foods was roughly flat, lifted by an Easter timing shift. Valentine’s Day fulfillment improved materially, but that operational success did not reverse the broader revenue decline. External pressures from tariffs, commodity inflation, and intense competition persist, and a large non-cash goodwill impairment signals lowered near‑term expectations for certain assets.

Management strategy and execution: Management has accelerated cost savings beyond initial targets, applied AI‑driven efficiencies, and stabilized the key Valentine’s Day period, narrowing adjusted losses despite the top‑line drop. A shift toward more balanced marketing investment is planned for the fourth quarter. Execution credibility is improving, yet the path to sustainable revenue growth remains uncertain, and the impairment charge reflects a reset of past acquisition value as the team builds a leaner, more reliable operating base.
