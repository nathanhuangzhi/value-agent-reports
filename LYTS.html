Management strategy and execution: Leadership completed the Royston acquisition while maintaining the dividend, signaling confidence in cash generation. A vertical market strategy and disciplined integration have yielded consistent earnings beats. Prudent capital management, even with acquisition-related leverage, and a clear focus on high-growth verticals strengthen management’s credibility and support the positive narrative around expanding the business.
