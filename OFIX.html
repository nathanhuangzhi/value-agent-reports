INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Core spine fixation and the Therapeutic Solutions (BGT) segment are growing at an above-market pace, with the top spine distributor partnerships delivering particularly strong momentum. The recent discontinuation of the M6 product line created a headline headwind, but pro forma trends show underlying demand remains resilient across U.S. spine, fracture, and BGT. External factors like one fewer selling day partially masked organic progress, and the stock’s sharp recent decline appears driven by sentiment rather than deterioration in commercial fundamentals.

Management strategy and execution: Management acted decisively to restructure the spine commercial channel and exit the M6 line, moves that are now translating into steady execution and outsize growth from key distributors. By reaffirming full-year 2026 guidance despite market pressure, leadership signaled confidence in the turnaround trajectory and the strategy’s ability to deliver profitable growth. The investor presentation underscores a sharpened focus on commercial productivity, while the market’s initial negative reaction has created what some analysts view as a tactical reset in expectations.
