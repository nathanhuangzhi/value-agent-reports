INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-04-16

Business & market dynamics: The company holds a strong regulatory position with the broadest portfolio of FDA-cleared exoskeleton indications and a landmark Medicare reimbursement decision for its Indego Personal device, which could unlock a large at-home market. However, the commercial payoff has been slow. Order delays and an uneven demand environment have led to volatile quarterly results, with a Q3 recovery followed by a sharp anticipated sequential decline in Q4, underscoring that converting the reimbursement tailwind into consistent, profitable adoption remains a primary challenge.

Management strategy and execution: In response to persistent financial strain, management launched a strategic business review after the Q3 2025 report, signaling a potential operational reset. The company closed a substantial private placement to address liquidity, which is critical given the auditor’s going concern warning. While this capital infusion provides a runway, the need for such a raise and the steep guided Q4 revenue drop highlight significant execution gaps. The market’s cautious optimism, reflected in some analyst price-target increases, hinges on whether the review yields a credible path to sustainable unit economics.
