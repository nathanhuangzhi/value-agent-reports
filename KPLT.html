INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-07

Business & market dynamics: Katapult’s core lease-to-own platform continues to show resilience, with gross originations growth driven by non-home-furnishings categories as consumers shift spending patterns. The nonprime consumer backdrop remains strained, evidenced by holiday-season softness, yet the company is seeing improving customer lifetime value and repeat rates. The pending merger with Aaron’s and CCF Holdings is poised to redefine the competitive landscape, combining e-commerce and omnichannel capabilities to serve a broad nonprime audience.

Management strategy and execution: CEO Orlando Zayas has steered the company toward consistent operational improvements, delivering a sharp profitability turn and strengthening the balance sheet. The team’s focus is now squarely on closing the transformative merger, which will fold Katapult into a larger, diversified platform while giving current shareholders a minority stake. Execution is centered on integration readiness and maintaining standalone momentum, with the merger seen as the primary catalyst to unlock scale and cross-channel synergies.
