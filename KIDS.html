Steady U.S. demand combines with fast-scaling overseas revenue, broadening the geographic mix. A recent FDA clearance for a small mini system further expands its niche pediatric addressable market. The company remains in a growth-investment phase and has not yet reached consistent profitability, but underlying demand signals are strengthening.

Management strategy and execution: The leadership team demonstrated confidence by raising full-year revenue guidance after a Q1 earnings beat and reaffirming its profitability objectives. It delivered a milestone quarter of positive free cash flow in late 2025 and is now steering toward full-year free cash flow breakeven in 2026, supported by tighter spending discipline. A steady cadence of product launches and credible interim achievements are building market confidence in management’s execution.
