INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-08

Business & market dynamics: DTI experienced a seasonally soft start to the year, with tool rental demand dampened by lower North American land activity and an early Canadian spring breakup. Ongoing pricing pressure persisted on the rental side. Offsetting this, product sales showed early recovery as Middle East inventories depleted, pointing to improving international demand for Deep Casing Tools. Management characterized the quarter’s trends as anticipated, with the overall activity environment aligning with the typical first-quarter trough and setting up for a stronger remainder of the year.

Management strategy and execution: The company completed its transition to a fully independent public entity, complete with a refreshed board, signaling a new phase of governance. Leadership reaffirmed the full-year 2026 outlook for consolidated revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted free cash flow, underscoring confidence in recovery despite the loss-making first quarter. Actions focused on navigating near-term pricing headwinds and seasonal slowness while positioning the rental-focused platform to capture growth as activity picks up in its core onshore and offshore horizontal drilling markets.
