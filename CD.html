Business & market dynamics: Robust demand for AI-related hardware is driving strong sales momentum, particularly from corporate and public sector clients building out infrastructure. However, this product mix shift toward lower-margin hardware is compressing gross margins, creating a tension between volume growth and profitability. The competitive landscape is intensifying, with OEMs and e-commerce rivals adding pressure. While the broader IT spending environment remains supportive, the margin dynamics tied to this hardware-heavy mix are a central watchpoint for the business’s near-term profile.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has launched the “Geared for Growth” program, a deliberate pivot toward higher-margin technical services and AI advisory, targeting meaningful margin improvement over the next two years. The company continues to return significant capital to shareholders, underscoring confidence in its financial position. Still, execution risk and the timeline for this service-mix transformation weigh on sentiment, with the market awaiting clearer evidence that the pivot can offset hardware margin headwinds and restore a more favorable profit architecture.
