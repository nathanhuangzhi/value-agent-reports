Business & market dynamics: Core operations are under pressure from a post-pandemic revenue reset, but the company is betting on a second-half rebound driven by a pivot into precision medicine. Recent acquisitions and a pharmacogenetics partnership with Foundation Medicine signal a deliberate shift toward higher-margin diagnostic services. Market sentiment has cooled sharply, with analysts slashing price targets, though the narrative frames this as a recalibration of growth expectations rather than a structural decline.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership reaffirmed full-year guidance and backed it with aggressive share repurchases, demonstrating near-term conviction. The deliberate deployment of a substantial cash reserve into targeted M&A and partnerships underscores a plan to reinvent the diagnostics platform. However, execution missteps have dented credibility, and the market’s willingness to buy into the turnaround now hinges on visible progress in the second half.
