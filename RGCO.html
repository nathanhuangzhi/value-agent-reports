INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-27

Business & market dynamics: An interim rate increase is bolstering the regulated gas utility’s margins, and equity earnings from the Mountain Valley Pipeline continue to grow. Offsetting this are declining throughput volumes, the departure of a major industrial customer, and a damaged LNG facility that will be out of service next winter, clouding near‑term demand.

Management strategy and execution: Management delivered strong earnings gains from rate relief and pipeline investments while candidly acknowledging emerging risks. Their proactive pursuit of interim rate adjustments and transparent discussion of customer loss and the LNG outage have preserved credibility, earning an analyst upgrade to Buy and reflecting confidence in their ability to manage through mixed operating conditions.
