AI/HPC datacenter plans advance with a 60MW interconnect and a Mississippi load study, yet the segment has no revenue. The company operates under a going concern warning, and AI demand remains unproven, making the transition story contingent on future execution.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is driving a shift to AI infrastructure, reducing debt and selling non-core assets while advancing interconnection and site development. However, a large shelf registration looms, threatening severe dilution relative to the company’s thin equity base. Debt restructuring pushed out maturities at higher cost, and the lack of AI revenue undermines confidence. The pivot remains highly speculative, and the going concern warning lingers.
