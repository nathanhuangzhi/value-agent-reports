INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: International demand is expanding rapidly, fueled by new tenders and entry into Eastern Europe, Latin America, and Asia Pacific, while the U.S. business remains under structural pressure as the long-term oxygen therapy market shifts toward portable oxygen concentrators. This transition is depressing direct-to-consumer and rental volumes and tilting the domestic mix toward B2B channels. Despite the U.S. headwind, overall unit volumes are rising and the company has reached positive adjusted EBITDA profitability, signaling an improving trajectory.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has been reinforced with the appointment of a new CFO with deep global device experience and refreshed governance. The team is executing a profitability-first playbook, having delivered on its commitment to turn adjusted EBITDA positive and beat its own internal revenue expectations. International commercial execution has been a clear bright spot, while the U.S. channel transition remains a work in progress. Recent updates and the profitability milestone are gradually rebuilding market credibility around the turnaround narrative.
