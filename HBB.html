Business & market dynamics: The core U.S. consumer business faces softening demand and cautious spending amid macroeconomic uncertainty, leading to lower volumes. However, the company is benefiting from a structural margin uplift via its foreign trade zone, with an additional one-time boost from an IEEPA-related inventory sell-through. The top-line pressure reflects consumer headwinds, but the margin trajectory signals improved operational resilience. Revenue decline stemmed entirely from volume weakness, not pricing erosion, indicating the brand is holding its own in a challenged discretionary environment.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is absorbing the demand softness while actively reinvesting for growth, with plans to ramp advertising and absorb accelerated ERP depreciation, which will weigh on near-term reported profit. Management reiterated full-year revenue growth expectations, suggesting confidence in new product launches and expanding retail distribution. The team is also navigating an uncertain but potentially large IEEPA refund. The market reacted negatively to the outlook, highlighting skepticism around the spending ramp and the one-off nature of the margin beat, though execution on cost and margin management remains credible.
