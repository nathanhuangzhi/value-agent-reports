Business & market dynamics: A tough luxury housing market is compressing core brokerage activity, with shifting commission splits and the divested property management unit further reducing top‑line contributions. Partially offsetting this, the company is cultivating a large development marketing pipeline — heavily weighted toward Florida — and has launched offices in Canada, France and the Caribbean, pivoting toward more project-based, geographically diversified revenue streams that are still in early days.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is reshaping the firm into a leaner, pure‑play luxury brand via strict expense controls, technology investments, and aggressive top‑agent recruitment. The debt‑free balance sheet supports international expansion, and management’s confident long‑term narrative, together with the visible development pipeline, has sustained market optimism despite deepening near‑term losses.
