INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: The company is riding strong operating momentum, with its wholesale channel powering broad-based growth as packaged coffee and ready-to-drink distribution gains translate into accelerating shelf presence and share. Direct-to-consumer returned to its best growth in over four years, while grocery-channel sales more than doubled. The business is successfully navigating ongoing coffee-market volatility and consumer pressure, outperforming broader category growth and signaling resilient end-market demand.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a clear playbook of distribution expansion, pack-size innovation, and disciplined pricing, while the Black Rifle Energy launch added incremental traction. Management raised its full-year outlook, reflecting confidence in the trajectory, and delivered a sharp profitability turnaround that moved the company to breakeven. The improved credibility follows a year of meeting or exceeding prior guidance, demonstrating better operational control and a stabilizing direct-to-consumer engine.
