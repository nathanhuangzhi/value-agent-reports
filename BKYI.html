Its multi-modal fingerprint, facial, and palm technology differentiates the company in an expanding zero-trust market, helping to build a growing base of recurring revenue. The recent Nasdaq relisting — following the resolution of compliance issues — signals renewed investor appetite for pure-play identity-centric cybersecurity plays amid heightened focus on identity threats and regulatory tailwinds.

Regaining Nasdaq compliance and resuming trading under a discretionary panel monitor until mid‑2027 demonstrates restored listing credibility, though the monitor signals ongoing exchange oversight. The narrative centers on stabilization and a credible pivot toward sustainable operations.
