Business & market dynamics: Core personal care franchises exhibit resilient demand backed by consumer loyalty, though reported results are clouded by restructuring noise. The stock’s climb to a new 52-week high reflects a technical re-rating from a deeply discounted valuation as investors begin to price in improving margin visibility. Analyst commentary remains mixed, with a price-target raise from UBS contrasting with a lingering sell rating elsewhere, signaling cautious rather than unbridled optimism.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a transformative portfolio overhaul, shedding the lower-quality Feminine Care unit and consolidating production capacity to improve balance-sheet flexibility. These moves are designed to drive a structural margin uplift and refocus the business around its strongest franchises. The market’s response—a higher price target and a new high—suggests growing confidence in the turnaround blueprint, even as near-term GAAP earnings remain weighed down by divestiture and transition costs.
