EMEA showed strength, but Asia-Pacific softened. A new collaboration with Basecamp Research for a vast genomic atlas signals future sequencing demand, and the SPRQ‑Nx beta launch is drawing positive early interest, pointing to broadening opportunities despite near‑term instrument softness.

Management strategy and execution: Management is leaning into clinical expansion and readying the SPRQ‑Nx commercial launch, while cutting operating expenses and using an IP sale to bolster the balance sheet. However, Vega instrument sales fell short of expectations, and pricing promotions reflect execution bumps. The team is balancing near‑term pressure with strategic bets on large‑scale projects and product innovation.
