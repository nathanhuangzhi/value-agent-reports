Business & market dynamics: Underlying demand signals are encouraging, with double-digit consumer traffic growth, improved new customer acquisition, and strong momentum in Europe’s e-commerce channel. However, a temporary operational disruption from a U.S. distribution center upgrade caused shipment delays that masked this demand and pressured quarterly results. External tariff costs and new royalty obligations from the joint venture further weighed on profitability. The core narrative is one of resilient brand interest and geographic expansion outside the U.S., albeit offset in the near term by self-inflicted logistics friction and macro cost headwinds.

Management strategy and execution: The leadership team, having resolved the warehouse disruption, is now pivoting to capitalize on the underlying demand. The transformative WHP Global joint venture closed, generating a one‑time gain that enabled full term‑loan repayment and a new $100 million share repurchase authorization. While full‑year guidance signaled confidence in a rebound, the earnings outlook fell short of market expectations, reflecting ongoing tariff and royalty pressures. The overall execution narrative is a mixed picture of a balance‑sheet reset and shareholder‑friendly capital moves, tempered by operational stumbles that tested near‑term credibility.
