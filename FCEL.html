INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-18

Business & market dynamics: While near-term backlog shrank, the sales pipeline exploded, fueled almost entirely by AI data center customers seeking clean baseload power. The company also began shipping carbon capture modules to Rotterdam, demonstrating international traction. This pivot has ignited a bullish narrative, cemented by an analyst upgrade that spotlighted the huge addressable opportunity in AI infrastructure power.

Management strategy and execution: Management is reorienting production around scalable, standardized power blocks for data centers and expanding Torrington capacity to support this shift. Early execution markers—a rapidly growing AI-driven pipeline and carbon capture modules heading to Europe—are building market credibility, evidenced by a swift post-sell-off analyst upgrade. Still, the initial earnings drop exposed lingering doubts about how quickly the pipeline will translate into firm backlog.
