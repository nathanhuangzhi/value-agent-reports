Despite near-term pressure, the company’s full-year outlook points to a significantly stronger second half, suggesting internal confidence that product and merchandising adjustments will restore traction. No explicit macro or competitive disruptions were cited; the weakness appears largely self-inflicted and tied to product execution rather than a deteriorating end-market environment.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership acknowledged the disappointing start but reaffirmed full-year guidance, signaling conviction in a gradual, sequential recovery. The CEO emphasized a measured approach, balancing speed with careful deliberation as the organization evolves its strategy and product assortment. The decision to stick with the annual outlook, even after a volatile quarter, supports a narrative of a management team that is deliberately navigating a turnaround—and that the market still finds credible.
