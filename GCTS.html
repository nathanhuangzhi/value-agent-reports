Business & market dynamics: After two years of declines, GCT returned to revenue growth, helped by a one‑time license fee while 5G chipset shipments accelerated sequentially. The customer base expanded from a handful to eight end users, and a reference‑platform deal with a major satellite provider anchors its pitch into AI‑edge and non‑terrestrial networks. This positions the company in high‑demand niches, but early‑stage product mix and customer concentration are still apparent.

Management strategy and execution: Management guides for a product‑led sales ramp and points to forthcoming new orders and customer announcements, hinting at design‑win traction. Credibility remains strained, however, by years of prior contraction, heavy ongoing losses, negative equity, substantial shareholder dilution, and a dangerously thin cash runway. The market has reacted sharply to positive headlines, but the rally reflects speculative optimism far ahead of a proven, sustainable operational turnaround.
