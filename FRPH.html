INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-16

Business & market dynamics: The portfolio’s performance is bifurcated. Mining royalties exhibit clear momentum with rising volumes and higher revenue per ton, reflecting healthy end-market demand. However, the multifamily and industrial segments are under pressure, with occupancy softening notably. The industrial portfolio is grappling with a significant vacancy, particularly a large Chelsea warehouse, dragging segment performance, while the multifamily portfolio in the DC area faces leasing headwinds. This creates a mixed picture: a strong natural resource royalty stream offset by short-term pain in core real estate operations that need to be leased up.

Management strategy and execution: Management is executing a deliberate pivot from platform expansion to operational harvesting. After a period of scaling—including the Altman Logistics acquisition—the focus has shifted entirely toward leasing execution and project stabilization. The leadership frame 2026 as the year to convert built capacity and embedded development value into dependable, recurring cash flow. Capital allocation is disciplined, with ample liquidity on hand to support this transition without distress. The market narrative now hinges on management’s credibility in delivering on the leasing push and turning vacant spaces into income-producing assets.
