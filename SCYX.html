INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-12

Business & market dynamics: SCYNEXIS is advancing a dual-pronged pipeline: it is progressing SCY-247, an intravenous antifungal with a Phase 1 readout expected in the second half of 2026 and an upcoming expanded access program that signals real-world need for new anti-infectives. The recent acquisition of SCY-770 adds an oral AMPK activator for autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease, a rare disease indication with a Phase 2 proof-of-concept study set to begin in late 2026. The company remains entirely clinical-stage, without commercial revenues, but a non-dilutive payment from GSK validates its legacy antifungal assets.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a transformative pipeline pivot through the SCY-770 acquisition and secured fresh capital to extend the cash runway, absorbing a one-time in-process R&D charge while increasing development spend. The team is hitting operational milestones—dosing the first IV patient and preparing an expanded access program—and has articulated clear catalysts through 2027. The combination of timely financings, a licensing payment from GSK, and pipeline diversification suggests disciplined capital allocation and steady execution against the strategic plan.
