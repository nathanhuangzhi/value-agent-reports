Business & market dynamics: Arq’s core activated carbon purification business is expanding sales volumes, although product mix shifts are creating pricing headwinds. The granular activated carbon (GAC) line is under strategic review, with a decision expected by the third quarter, signaling a potential pivot to higher-margin offerings. The company is navigating a transitional phase, edging closer to breakeven with improving operational profitability, while the end-market demand backdrop remains steady.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership reaffirmed full-year guidance and highlighted the completion of a biennial plant turnaround under budget, showcasing cost discipline. A strategic optimization review of GAC is underway, and the CEO pointed to strength in the powdered activated carbon segment as a profitability driver. High insider ownership suggests management alignment with shareholders as the company methodically works toward sustained positive earnings.
