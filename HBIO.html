Business & market dynamics: Revenue was pressured by soft academic and Asia-Pacific distributor demand, partially offset by biopharma growth and early traction in new translational-science platforms like MeshMEA organoid, BTX electroporation, and SoHo telemetry. Gross margins benefited from a favorable product mix shift, though inventory buildup strained cash flow. The demand environment remains mixed, with pharma strength counterbalancing persistent academic and international weakness.

Management strategy and execution: Under relatively new CEO Jim Green and permanent CFO Mark Frost, the company is executing Project Viking—a manufacturing consolidation and recurring-revenue push. Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, projecting modest growth, and pointed to encouraging adoption of new products. Restructuring remains on track, lending early credibility to the turnaround, but the strategy’s success hinges on reviving demand in lagging academic and Asia-Pacific channels.
