INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-19

Business & market dynamics: The preferred equity is under clear pressure, trading meaningfully below its intended par value as investor demand weakens. This persistent discount reflects market skepticism about the sustainability of the high-yield dividend promise, especially as the underlying bitcoin acquisition model shows strain. The funding loop that once supported aggressive BTC buying is cracking, with the instrument’s price signaling a need for higher risk compensation. Added competitive threats from new bitcoin-linked products and a cautious crypto sentiment are further clouding demand, calling into question whether this perpetual can reliably serve as a low-cost financing vehicle.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is attempting to shore up confidence by adjusting dividend policy—pausing payouts and shifting to a semi-monthly cadence—but the market has not bought into the fixes. The decision to sell bitcoin for the first time in years to fund obligations contradicts the long-standing “never sell” ethos and raises real concerns about cash-flow management. Director insider selling adds to worries over commitment. Overall, the strategic choice to rely on this perpetual as an ATM-like funding mechanism is now openly questioned, and management’s ability to restore investor faith in the instrument’s stability and the broader capital-allocation framework appears deeply eroded.
