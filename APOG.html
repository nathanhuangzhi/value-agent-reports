Multiple institutional investors added to or initiated positions during the quarter, signaling confidence in the underlying demand backdrop and the strategic direction.

The team reaffirmed fiscal-year guidance and kept the deal on track for an early second-quarter close, reinforcing credibility around disciplined capital allocation and operational follow-through. The market’s reception to the deal and the steady guidance posture underscore management’s effective execution narrative.
