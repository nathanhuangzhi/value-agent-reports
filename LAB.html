INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Standard BioTools is seeing accelerating momentum in consumables, the recurring-revenue engine of its life-science tools portfolio, while capital-equipment sales remain subdued in a constrained spending environment. The divestiture of the SomaLogic proteomics business to Illumina removes a dilutive, non-core operation and intensifies focus on single-cell and spatial biology—a competitive arena where 10x Genomics and Illumina loom large. NIH funding uncertainties and cautious instrument purchasing across academia and biopharma persist as external headwinds, though the consumables trajectory signals strengthening customer engagement with the installed base.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a transformative liquidity event via the SomaLogic sale, securing ample cash to de-risk the balance sheet and pivot toward inorganic growth. Operational restructuring is evident in sharply narrowed losses and a clear path to adjusted EBITDA positivity by year-end, underscoring cost discipline. The full-year guidance hold, despite divestiture, projects stability in the core franchise, while the explicit emphasis on acquisitions signals an appetite to reshape the company’s scale and competitive position through disciplined capital deployment rather than relying solely on organic expansion.
