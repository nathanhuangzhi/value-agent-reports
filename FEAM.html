INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-03

Business & market dynamics: FEAM is a pre-revenue developer of domestic boron and lithium assets, benefiting from tightening boron supply-demand fundamentals and its designation as a U.S. critical mineral supplier. Commercial traction is emerging through non-binding offtake indications of interest for boric acid and calcium sulphate from domestic industrial users, though these remain contingent on product qualification and definitive agreements. The macro environment is supportive, with policy tailwinds favoring domestic supply chains, but the business has yet to demonstrate consistent production or convert interest into binding revenue.

Management strategy and execution: After narrowly avoiding a liquidity crisis, management executed a deeply dilutive emergency equity raise that secured near-term survival at the cost of existing shareholders. The focus has since pivoted to achieving project milestones, securing non-dilutive federal funding, and advancing customer offtake negotiations toward a Final Investment Decision. Credibility is fragile: while cost discipline and early commercial outreach show progress, the team must now deliver binding contracts and project financing to transition from a development-stage narrative to execution, a path that remains unproven.
