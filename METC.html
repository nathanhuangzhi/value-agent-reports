INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-16

Business & market dynamics: The core met coal business is under pressure from weak global steel demand and declining export prices, eroding near-term profitability and driving the stock to 52-week lows. Meanwhile, the Brook critical minerals project is gaining notice, shifting the narrative toward a long-term rare-earths optionality. This creates a mixed picture: near-term cyclical headwinds with a speculative long-term growth asset.

Management strategy and execution: Management has pivoted from a disciplined low-cost coal operator to a development-focused critical minerals company, raising significant capital through share issuances and convertible notes to fund Brook’s advancement. This move strengthened liquidity but introduced dilution risk, and the market questions whether project returns will outpace dilution. Near-term deteriorating results are testing leadership credibility, raising the bar for Brook execution milestones.
