The disciplined build-out of a productive direct sales force and consistent execution on regulatory milestones underscore operational competence. While the analyst consensus is largely constructive with multiple recent upgrades, a lingering sell rating and a recent share-price pullback suggest some market skepticism persists, though the overarching narrative is one of reliable execution and improving financial sustainability.
