INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-08

Business & market dynamics: The company is shifting away from migrant-related programs, with strong underlying growth in virtual care, mobile phlebotomy, and medical transportation—transportation reached record levels, and the SteadyMD platform is scaling rapidly. Secular tailwinds favoring in-home, tech-enabled care and supportive regulatory/payer partnerships underpin the growth narrative, though the complete absence of migrant revenue creates a year-over-year headwind that masks the core momentum.

Management strategy and execution: Management is pivoting the business from emergency response toward a scalable, proactive care delivery model, integrating SteadyMD and Care Anywhere to drive efficiency. Revenue guidance was raised on robust core demand, but adjusted EBITDA guidance was left unchanged, reflecting persistent losses and margin pressure from fuel costs and growth investments. The path to profitability remains a key challenge, testing leadership's execution credibility.
