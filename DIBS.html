INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-21

Business & market dynamics: Demand remains soft, with the U.S. housing market acting as a persistent headwind for the luxury online marketplace. Active buyers have declined, and overall GMV has slipped, though the company expects a return to year-over-year GMV growth by the fourth quarter. International expansion and AI-driven product enhancements represent potential catalysts, but cautious consumer behavior and a discounted valuation reflect markets’ severe downside expectations.

Management strategy and execution: Management is pursuing deliberate cost discipline, pulling back sales and marketing spend to bolster profitability while continuing to invest in product and engineering. The shift has delivered positive adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow, and the company has used its strong cash position to repurchase shares, signaling confidence. However, the lack of forward guidance and recent top-line softness keep market conviction tentative, with credibility hinging on hitting the promised GMV inflection later in the year.
