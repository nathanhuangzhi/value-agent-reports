A third-party risk snapshot, however, reveals a dangerously thin cash position—less than a year’s runway—for this sub-$10 million company, and notes extreme share-price volatility and recent shareholder dilution, tempering any topical growth story.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership strikes a positive tone on operational progress and narrowing losses, but credibility is strained by its repeated reliance on dilutive equity raises. The recent public offering, meant to shore up a weak balance sheet, further diluted existing holders and sent shares sharply lower—a pattern flagged in independent risk checks. While top-line momentum reflects some execution capability, the capital-allocation approach raises concerns about the sustainability of financing and long-term shareholder alignment.
