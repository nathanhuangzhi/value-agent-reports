INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-24

Business & market dynamics: The narrative is shifting from a broad hardware vendor to a focused public-safety and software play, anchored by the Allerium platform and next-generation 9-1-1 (NG911) demand. While this positions the company for secular tailwinds in mission-critical infrastructure, near‑term momentum is mixed: sales remain under pressure and bookings were soft, even as a sizable backlog offers conversion potential.

Management strategy and execution: Management is delivering on a multi‑pillar turnaround—divesting the satellite business to sharpen focus, achieving five straight quarters of positive operating cash flow, and narrowing losses. The team has built some credibility by beating estimates and consistently improving operational discipline, though the market still needs proof that the portfolio transition and backlog will translate into durable top‑line growth.
