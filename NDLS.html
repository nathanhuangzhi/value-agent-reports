INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-07

Business & market dynamics: The company is experiencing a clear operational rebound, with robust comparable sales growth and rising average unit volumes driven by better in-store execution, a revamped menu, and disciplined marketing. Guest satisfaction has climbed meaningfully, signaling a strengthened customer experience. Simultaneously, a footprint rationalization is underway, closing underperforming locations while sales transfer favorably to nearby units — a sign that underlying brand demand remains resilient even as the physical base contracts.

Management strategy and execution: Management raised its full-year guidance, targeting free-cash-flow positivity and reflecting increased confidence in the turnaround. The strategy centers on operational rigor, targeted marketing, and a deliberate program of restaurant closures to shed unprofitable sites. Execution is gaining credibility after a beat-and-raise quarter, but the balance sheet remains heavily leveraged with tight liquidity. The path forward hinges on sustaining the improved trajectory at remaining stores while navigating the constraints of a debt-laden capital structure.
