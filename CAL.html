INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-05

Business & market dynamics: Brand Portfolio momentum is strong, led by organic growth in lead brands and the Stuart Weitzman acquisition, driving margin expansion and market share gains. Famous Footwear, however, continues to see declining comparable sales and remains a drag. The business is supported by a large direct-to-consumer base, but mixed retail trends keep performance bifurcated.

Management strategy and execution: Management raised full-year guidance, reflecting confidence in brand-driven profitability. The focus is on scaling Brand Portfolio and integrating Stuart Weitzman, though the acquisition also added leverage and profit pressure. The market remains cautious, awaiting sustained execution and a resolution to Famous Footwear’s weakness, viewing recent improvement as positive but not yet convincing.
