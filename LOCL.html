Demand signals remain healthy, and the operational focus is on yield-led growth from the installed base rather than new facility construction, suggesting a period of organic commercial expansion and deepening retail relationships.

The CEO recently sold a small number of shares solely to cover tax withholding obligations upon equity vesting, not a change in conviction. Management is actively advancing strategic retail partnerships and steering the company toward improved efficiency and reduced losses, reflecting a deliberate pivot from aggressive capital deployment to extracting greater productivity from existing assets. The tone from leadership is confident and execution-focused.
