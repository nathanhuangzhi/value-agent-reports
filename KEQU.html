A shrinking order backlog points to possible demand deceleration ahead. Management strategy and execution: Management is integrating the Nu Aire acquisition, reducing debt, and investing in compliance infrastructure. Despite pre-tax gains, higher taxes and minority interests weighed on net income, prompting a sharp stock sell-off. The subsequent share recovery above the 200-day moving average and a consensus Hold rating reflect cautious acknowledgment of long-term discipline against near-term integration and demand uncertainties.
