The narrative leans bullish, but the small-cap profile and consumer-discretionary exposure make the adoption ramp a closely watched execution story.

They have delivered improving gross margins and steady revenue progression, and they guide toward cash flow positivity in 2026, supported by sufficient liquidity and recent institutional funding. The upcoming corporate update call serves as a near-term test of retail sell-through traction and project deployment.
