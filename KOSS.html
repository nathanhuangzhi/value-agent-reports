Business & market dynamics: Modest top-line expansion was more than offset by deepening losses, as tariffs on Chinese-sourced inventory and earlier elevated freight costs severely eroded profitability. A notable bright spot is the rapid expansion of the higher-margin direct-to-consumer channel, which has become the largest segment, partially cushioning weak demand in Europe. Overall, the underlying business is navigating significant cost headwinds even as its channel mix shifts favorably toward more profitable sales.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership points to the DTC pivot as a deliberate strategic move and acknowledges the margin pressures from tariffs and logistics, but the investment narrative is almost entirely detached from operational execution. The stock has been swept up in intense meme-stock mania, fueled by social-media speculation around influencer Keith Gill rather than any fundamental catalyst. In this environment, management’s decisions on cost and channel are largely overshadowed by extreme, sentiment-driven trading dynamics.
