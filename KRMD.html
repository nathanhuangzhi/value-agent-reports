Business & market dynamics: The company’s core subcutaneous immunoglobulin (SCIg) business shows broad-based momentum, with domestic gains fueled by new patient starts and competitive account conversions, while international expansion is accelerating sharply. European prefilled syringe conversions and sizable distributor orders into new geographies, supported by Freedom60 MDR clearance, are driving outsized growth abroad. The non-immunoglobulin pipeline is deepening, signaling a growing addressable market beyond the current SCIg base. Demand for patient-centric large-volume infusion solutions remains robust, and the commercial organization is converting that interest into durable share gains across both established and emerging markets.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership delivered record quarterly results that exceeded expectations while keeping cash consumption minimal and adjusted EBITDA near break-even. Guidance was reiterated, reflecting confidence in sustaining top-line momentum, achieving full-year positive adjusted EBITDA, and generating cash flow. Strategic moves—regulatory submissions for new products, disciplined expense management, and a preserved debt facility—demonstrate a clear, credible path toward profitability. The team is executing a focused commercial strategy that balances domestic account conversions with high-return international opportunities, and its consistent delivery against targets is gradually building market trust in the turnaround narrative.
