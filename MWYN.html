However, market conviction is shaky: a sharp AI-news spike was accompanied by warnings of a bear trap, the stock subsequently drifted back into a low-volume range, and trading reflects uncertainty rather than sustained belief in the transformation.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a rapid strategic repositioning, launching NexaCore, acquiring DJ Mex, and committing to a physical battery-recycling plant—shifting from an asset-light model to operational participation in high-margin materials recovery. This ambition is undercut by credibility concerns: the company has a micro-cap structure, dilutive history, limited cash runway, and a stock price pattern that suggests market skepticism about the pivot’s long-term viability absent a structural break in volume and technicals.
