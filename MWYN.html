The AI-themed announcement triggered a sharp, volume-driven spike, but subsequent trading activity suggests limited conviction. The stock remains range-bound, with technical signals flashing oversold conditions and warning of a potential “trap.” Commentary highlights a lack of sustained follow-through, below-average volume, and a prevailing analyst “Sell” rating, painting a picture of a micro-cap story where the AI narrative has not yet translated into durable market interest.
