INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-14

Business & market dynamics: The company is assembling a portfolio of high-grade, smaller-scale gold and silver assets in North America, centered on a direct-shipping-ore (DSO) model that turns Manh Choh’s production into a self-funding platform for growth. Its recent merger with Dolly Varden adds meaningful silver exposure in the Kitsault Valley, while Johnson Tract and Lucky Shot provide development upside. The macro backdrop features a gold-silver bull market, and the elimination of hedge contracts now gives the firm full leverage to rising precious-metals prices, positioning it to benefit from continued momentum.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has moved aggressively to boost shareholder exposure by removing all gold hedges and replacing them with lower-cost debt, signaling confidence in the gold price trajectory. The strategy relies on the DSO hub-and-spoke approach: cash flow from Manh Choh funds exploration and advancement of the pipeline without heavy equity dilution. The Dolly Varden integration and plans for a 2027 production ramp reflect a deliberate build-out toward mid-tier producer scale. Credibility is rising as management delivers on promises, though operational dependence on Kinross and variability inherent to the DSO model remain acknowledged execution risks.
