INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-15

Business & market dynamics: AerSale is undergoing a mix shift, with declining whole asset sales masking underlying momentum in recurring revenue streams such as USM demand, leasing, and MRO services. While Q1 saw softer USM sales, MRO expansion and margin recovery provided a partial offset, reflecting a business transitioning toward more predictable earnings. The aviation aftermarket environment appears supportive, but elevated debt levels introduce sensitivity to interest expenses, and analyst sentiment remains cautious, with consensus pointing to a “Reduce” rating despite the stock’s discount to book value.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a deliberate pivot away from lumpy asset sales toward higher-margin, recurring activities—evidenced by MRO capacity expansion and a sharp reduction in feedstock acquisitions, which suggests more disciplined capital allocation. This shift is gradually improving profitability metrics, though the market remains skeptical, as reflected in neutral-to-hold ratings and lingering concerns about leverage. The strategy’s credibility hinges on the ability to sustain this margin recovery while managing debt risk, with current valuation providing a margin of safety that some deep-value investors find compelling.
