Hard macro conditions are challenging the home furnishings category broadly.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a deliberate pivot from a product-driven couch maker to a multi-room lifestyle brand, framing the transition as pivotal. The company is reinforcing confidence with a newly authorized share repurchase program. The market narrative is supported by recent institutional buying and a moderate buy consensus from analysts, who appear willing to look through near-term execution wobbles to the longer-term brand transformation strategy.
