Core demand is stable, with modest growth supported by ongoing showroom expansion. The brand’s adaptable Sactionals concept retains relevance, but the narrative reflects a business in transition rather than one enjoying broad-based demand acceleration.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a strategic shift toward a broader brand identity, emphasizing platform and ecosystem development. Capital allocation signals management’s conviction: a new share repurchase authorization has been added, backed by a debt-free balance sheet and strong cash generation. The guidance posture is measured—annual growth is expected but the early-year loss outlook suggests management is setting conservative near-term expectations while funding brand-building initiatives, a move that markets appear to accept for now.
