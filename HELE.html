INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-06

Business & market dynamics: Escalating tariffs on Chinese imports shattered the operating model, exposing an over-reliance on China sourcing and concentrated retail customers. This triggered a sharp organic sales decline and margin compression, forcing a strategic reset. The portfolio’s brands retain consumer recognition, but the business is now a high-risk turnaround story whose recovery depends on supply chain diversification and the drawdown of tariff-inflated inventory.

Management strategy and execution: New leadership is aggressively implementing dual sourcing to cut China exposure, aiming to restore margins as tariffed inventory clears. Capital allocation focuses on debt reduction using free cash flow, complemented by inventory cuts and asset sales. However, the massive non-cash goodwill impairments have severely dented credibility, raising doubts about past acquisition discipline. The market cautiously watches for evidence that the restructuring gains traction.
