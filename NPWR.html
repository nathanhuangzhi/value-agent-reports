INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-13

Business & market dynamics: Net Power remains pre-revenue, with its entire investment case resting on future commercialization of a natural gas power generation system with inherent carbon capture. There is no current operating asset or contracted cash flow; the company is still advancing Project Permian Phase I toward a final investment decision. Market attention is fixed on the pursuit of a long-term power purchase agreement, which is viewed as the critical unlock for project financing and broad deployment. Underlying demand for clean, firm, and low-cost electricity provides a supportive macro backdrop, but the narrative carries significant binary risk tied to offtake execution in a competitive and capital-intensive sector.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is actively working to bridge the gap from development concept to bankable contract. Engaging a strategic advisor to facilitate power offtake discussions marks a deliberate step to formalize customer commitments and maintain the project timeline. Management highlights in-house engineering capability and expertise in carbon capture as key differentiators, aiming to position natural gas as the lowest-cost clean firm power source. Until a binding offtake is announced, the market will continue to judge credibility on management’s ability to convert technical promise into de-risked, contracted revenue streams.
