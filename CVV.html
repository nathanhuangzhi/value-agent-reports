Business & market dynamics: After the SDC divestiture, CVV is a pure-play equipment provider to aerospace, semiconductor, and battery energy storage end markets. Continuing operations face significant revenue contraction and compressed profitability, yet order inflow is showing early signs of improvement. This mixed picture is tempered by cautious analyst sentiment, with ratings pointing to overvaluation concerns and lack of confidence that nascent demand can quickly translate into sustained recovery.

Management strategy and execution: Management completed the sale of the SDC division to Atlas Copco, transforming the company into a debt‑free entity with a fortified cash position. The move simplifies the corporate structure and grants strategic flexibility to refocus on the core CVD Equipment business. However, market credibility remains strained: the transaction initially sparked a sharp stock rally, but persistent sell‑side downgrades and skepticism that the move was merely a momentum event highlight the pressure on leadership to deliver a convincing operational turnaround.
