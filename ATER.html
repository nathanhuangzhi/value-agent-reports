INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-09

Business & market dynamics: The company is exiting its legacy e-commerce consumer brands entirely, pivoting into critical minerals and AI-driven exploration in Africa. This abrupt shift trades a struggling, unprofitable retail portfolio for exposure to tantalum and copper trading, aligning with rising battery-metal demand. While the core consumer business was cash‑negative, the new mineral venture remains unproven and highly speculative, with no operational history.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a binding sale of the entire brand portfolio, secured a strategic investment, and installed David Lazar as CEO to drive the transformation. They formed a joint venture with Lithosquare and a partnership with Wogen Resources, aiming to fund operations through non‑dilutive mineral trading and eliminate future equity dilution. The market initially cheered these moves, but the strategy’s success hinges on execution in unfamiliar, geopolitically sensitive markets.
