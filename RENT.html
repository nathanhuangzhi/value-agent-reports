INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-18

Business & market dynamics: Revenue momentum is solid, fueled by fast-growing add-on services that are outpacing core subscriptions. Yet subscriber acquisition remains moderate, seasonal patterns introduce choppiness, and the business continues to burn cash without reaching profitability. Operational strains from scaling, combined with a crowded competitive environment, add further pressure.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is in transition after the CEO and CFO exits; interim executives drawn from Nordstrom are now running the company while permanent appointments remain open. Reaffirmed full-year targets offer a fragile sense of continuity, but unresolved Nasdaq listing compliance issues and internal control weaknesses are clouding execution credibility, and the organizational vacuum elevates risk until a stable leadership team is settled.
