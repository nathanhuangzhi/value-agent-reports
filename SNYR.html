Business & market dynamics: Synergy is transitioning from a legacy brain-health brand into a functional beverage growth story, leveraging retail distribution in Walmart and Kroger. Consumer demand for focus and energy products is strong, but recent performance was hampered by temporary cash constraints, now resolved through a capital raise. A sharp rise in short interest and a sell rating signal market skepticism about the turnaround’s sustainability.

Management strategy and execution: Management fortified the balance sheet with an equity raise and gained approval for a reverse stock split to support Nasdaq listing. The strategic pivot is endorsed by a bullish analyst price target, but a separate upgrade to a lower sell rating reflects lingering doubts. Credibility hinges on execution of the beverage rollout.
