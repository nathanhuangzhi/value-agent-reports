Forward guidance implies a meaningful acceleration in revenue, signalling confidence in the mounting retail pipeline. While top‑line momentum is evident, the cost base has expanded faster, constraining net income and highlighting the challenge of managing expenses through this growth phase. With a modest cash cushion, the near‑term narrative revolves around converting distribution gains into more durable, profitable operations without further margin erosion.
