Custom‑studio and online growth are viewed as channels to strengthen consumer engagement and future demand.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is prioritizing organic growth by intensifying sales productivity in existing stores, selectively opening corporate‑owned locations, and acquiring licensee stores to improve store economics. Recent commentary stressed e‑commerce expansion and pricing moves to protect margins. Market reception to the growth roadmap is constructive, with narratives tying custom‑studio rollout and domestic production to longer‑term earnings improvement; however, thin dividend coverage is noted as a watchpoint.
