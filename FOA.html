This dynamic highlights the quarter‑to‑quarter volatility in the company’s fair‑value‑driven revenue model, even as end‑borrower appetite stays healthy.

The team delivered adjusted earnings above consensus, underscoring improved operational leverage. Commentary on the call emphasized a disciplined approach to originations and capital fortification, pointing to accelerating March volumes and a reinforced balance sheet as evidence that the company is well‑positioned to serve the growing modern‑retirement market.
