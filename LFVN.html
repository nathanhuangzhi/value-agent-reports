Business & market dynamics: The direct-selling business is under heavy strain as fading demand for the MindBody GLP-1 system slams North American sales and shrinks the consultant base. LoveBiome provides a partial offset, signaling a product pivot away from a waning trend. Asia Pacific and Europe are more stable, with little currency drag. The primary external force is the cooling of GLP-1-related wellness enthusiasm, causing a sharp demand reset and network contraction.

Management strategy and execution: Interim leadership issued a conservative, single-point guidance cut that markets interpreted as realistic (shares rose). The strategy centers on pivoting to LoveBiome and trying to halt consultant attrition. Execution risk remains high given the CEO transition and the ongoing network decline. The posture is defensive and stabilization-focused; restoring distributor momentum and establishing permanent leadership are the critical credibility tests ahead.
