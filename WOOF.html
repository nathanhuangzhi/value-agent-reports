INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Petco’s top line remains under pressure from fierce competition with Chewy and mass retailers, keeping comparable sales flat to declining. The company is gradually building momentum by pivoting toward higher‑margin services and premium food, repositioning itself as a comprehensive petcare destination. However, the burden of high leverage and a cautious consumer spending backdrop temper near‑term optimism, leaving the stock cheap after a steep selloff but overshadowed by debt concerns.

Management strategy and execution: Management’s “Reach for the Sky” plan targets service expansion, premium offerings, and footprint optimization. Margin discipline is visible in improving profitability despite soft sales, and fiscal‑year guidance implies stabilization, fueling a post‑earnings share surge. Nevertheless, analysts remain on hold, waiting for concrete evidence that the strategic pivot can deliver a sustainable turnaround before crediting execution. Elevated debt continues to loom as the primary risk, making this a high‑stakes bridge that depends entirely on follow‑through.
