The business is demonstrating volume-driven operating leverage, as an evolving payor mix and maturing manufacturing processes are lifting margins even while the top line advances, suggesting the model is scaling toward profitability.

The team is actively narrowing losses by controlling expenses and raising gross margins without sacrificing growth investments. The tone on the earnings call and the presence of several sell-side analysts signal that management is building credibility by consistently delivering on its operational milestones and guiding the company along a visible path to cash-flow breakeven.
