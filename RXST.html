INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: RxSight’s earlier rapid adoption momentum slowed in 2025, raising questions about the durability of growth. Recent signals point to stabilization: procedure volumes and Light Adjustable Lens (LAL) demand remain solid, supported by positive clinical data and international expansion, including new approvals in Asia and New Zealand. However, a sharp decline in sales of Light Delivery Devices (LDDs) suggests the capital-equipment cycle is weakening, which could constrain future lens uptake if the installed base doesn’t grow. The overall picture is mixed—steady end-market pull for the lens technology, but flagging device placements and a cautious sell-side stance highlight lingering growth-reacceleration risks.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is navigating a transitional period. The company reiterated its 2026 sales outlook and signaled confidence in stabilizing trends, while acknowledging higher operating expenses to fund strategic initiatives. A recent CFO transition introduces execution uncertainty during a critical phase. Analysts remain guarded, with neutral ratings and target prices clustered tightly, reflecting a wait-and-see posture. The market’s focus is squarely on whether management can convert early stabilization into a sustained commercial reacceleration, and the credibility of the current guidance hinges on delivering visible LDD sales recovery and consistent lens volume growth.
