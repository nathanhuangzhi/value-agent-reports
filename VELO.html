INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-11

Business & market dynamics: Velo3D is benefiting from surging space and defense demand, with its domestically produced Sapphire printers qualifying for sensitive NDAA-compliant programs. Strong defense logistics bookings and its role as a SpaceX supplier are driving robust revenue momentum and a positive gross-margin inflection. Yet the stock’s technical stalling amid this growth suggests caution about the dilution overhang, even as underlying demand catalysts appear durable.

Management strategy and execution: Management recently raised capital, sharply increasing the share count and pressuring per-share value. A new CFO and director are upgrading governance, while the focus is on cash discipline and backlog conversion. The full-year revenue guidance sets an ambitious bar, and the market is watching whether Q2 margins hold and whether dilution can be out-earned by accelerating top-line growth.
