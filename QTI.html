INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: QT Imaging is experiencing accelerating demand for its radiation-free breast scanners, reflected in record shipments and broadening international adoption. The business is pivoting from a pure hardware model toward a SaaS- and biomarker-driven platform, which promises recurring revenue and deeper clinical integration within a market receptive to non‑ionizing imaging alternatives.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a rapid turnaround, highlighted by a Nasdaq relisting and the resolution of legacy warrant liabilities. Management has affirmed ambitious forward guidance while recasting the company as a high‑margin, cloud‑enabled imaging franchise. The shift to contracted scanner commitments and a cloud SaaS platform signals a deliberate move toward more predictable, scalable growth and strengthens market credibility.
