INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-07

Business & market dynamics: Angi is undergoing a deliberate model transition, dismantling its high-volume Network business (which collapsed after homeowners were given greater choice) while Proprietary Service Requests surged and International revenue grew. Overall demand, measured by Service Requests, returned to growth. The rapid rise of AI and large language model platforms is reshaping customer acquisition, creating both a competitive threat and an opportunity to build a next-generation, AI-driven marketplace.

Management strategy and execution: New leadership halted financial guidance and launched an AI-native reorganization, freezing legacy development to concentrate resources on a platform built around AI agents. A COO was promoted, the workforce was reduced, and the balance sheet was strengthened through discounted debt buybacks. These aggressive moves frame a high-conviction technological bet, but the withdrawal of near-term visibility and sweeping operational changes amplify execution uncertainty.
