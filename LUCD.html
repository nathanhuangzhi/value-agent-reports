An expanded Uber partnership targeting 35,000+ vehicles aims to unlock a far larger addressable market. While orders and revenue are growing, supplier‑driven February delivery disruptions suggest operational fragility. The stock’s decline despite these positive updates signals persistent market skepticism around scaling and competitive execution.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership raised substantial capital from PIF, a public offering, and Uber, framing a disciplined path to profitability through recurring software/mobility revenue, manufacturing efficiency, and cost reduction. The new Atlas drive unit and detailed midsize roadmap underscore technical ambition. However, the market’s lukewarm reception to the capital raise and operational progress points to credibility concerns—investors appear wary of dilution risk and the company’s ability to deliver on ambitious targets without further stumbles.
