Business & market dynamics: Revenue weakness stemmed from a soft retail environment and disruption during a full product-line reset. Demand improved in March as new products rolled out and legacy inventory was cleared, though the bulk of sales benefits will accrue later. The business remains under pressure from an elevated cost base and an ongoing transition, with no sustained demand recovery yet confirmed.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a product overhaul, leveraging a celebrity partnership, and shoring up liquidity. An amended credit agreement provided temporary covenant relief and new financing, easing immediate bankruptcy concerns. However, the company had previously warned of substantial doubt about its viability, and the market reads recent moves as short-term crisis management. Execution risk remains high as the team works to stabilize the business.
