INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-16

Business & market dynamics: Core procedure revenue faces near‑term disruption from a voluntary hormone pellet recall, which pulled sales force attention away from clinic activity and dampened both existing‑clinic volume and new‑clinic productivity. The dietary supplement line, however, is gaining healthy traction through e‑commerce, providing a partial offset. Underlying demand for bioidentical hormone replacement therapy appears intact, and the contributor narrative points to a supportive regulatory backdrop. The business appears to be weathering a transitory operational setback while leaning on supplement growth as procedure momentum resets.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is deliberately rebuilding the sales force—expansion is reported to be nearing the targeted level—and has telegraphed a return to procedure growth in the second half of the year. The recall is being treated as a one‑time distraction, and the organization is pivoting back toward driving new‑clinic activations. Analyst reactions are split: Truist upgraded to a strong‑buy on the recovery thesis, while others trimmed price targets. Credibility now depends on whether the enlarged sales team can convert the expected pipeline into a sustained procedure rebound over the coming quarters.
