The narrative hinges on a drone supercycle and a shift toward higher‑margin software and services, though actual customer adoption has yet to materialize at scale.

Management strategy and execution: Management is driving the transformation with new product introductions and an at‑the‑market equity filing to fund the pivot, which raises dilution concerns. Analyst upgrades to strong‑buy contrast with a lingering sell rating and an AI‑panel bearish view on execution and funding risk. Market credibility now depends on converting early platform wins into recurring service revenue and on delivering the drone ramp without further shareholder dilution.
