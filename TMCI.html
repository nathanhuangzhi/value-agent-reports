INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Core bunion-procedure revenue is under pressure, though quarterly results modestly beat subdued expectations. Early uptake of new implant systems is gradually broadening surgeon adoption, and favorable FDA regulatory milestones combined with positive clinical data have injected some market enthusiasm. Still, the underlying demand environment remains soft, and the business continues to post losses. Upcoming launches—SuperBite, SpeedXM, and Lapiplasty Lightning—are intended to expand the product portfolio and re-ignite growth, but the near‑term outlook is one of cautious stabilization rather than clear momentum.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership reaffirmed full-year revenue and adjusted-EBITDA guidance while sharply cutting cash-consumption expectations through disciplined operating-expense reductions. The focus is squarely on executing the product pipeline to attract both new and existing Lapiplasty surgeons. Credibility hinges on delivering against this plan: meeting the tempered guidance and demonstrating that new launches can translate into durable commercial traction, even as the business operates well below breakeven.
