Business & market dynamics: Stoneridge is showing early signs of a turnaround, with demand driven by its MirrorEye camera-monitor system (surpassing 150,000 units) and growth in Brazilian OEM and off-highway markets. This product-led momentum is partly offset by persistent commercial vehicle end-market weakness. The sequential improvement in sales and margins points to a business stabilizing after prior declines, though the company remains in a net loss position, reflecting ongoing cyclical pressures in its core truck and automotive supplier markets.

Management strategy and execution: Under CEO Natalia Noblet and a new interim CFO, management is focused on operational execution and cost discipline, citing improved manufacturing and quality initiatives, as well as net tariff recoveries. They raised full-year revenue guidance and highlighted continued program awards for MirrorEye and electronic controls, signaling confidence in the innovation pipeline. The market is beginning to reflect this narrative, with technical indicators turning positive, though credibility will hinge on sustaining the sequential margin gains and converting early product wins into profitable growth.
