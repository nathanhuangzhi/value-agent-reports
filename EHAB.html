INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-07

Business & market dynamics: Enhabit’s core home health segment posted modest gains, while hospice delivered stronger growth. A fiduciary litigation settlement provided a non‑recurring cash inflow, and exit funds from the VitalCaring partnership add non‑operating benefits. The home health and hospice industry has consolidated rapidly, leaving Enhabit as the last public pure‑play—a situation that drew activist attention and now anchors a take‑private narrative.

Management strategy and execution: Management agreed to an all‑cash acquisition by Kinderhook, but a competing bidder has submitted higher offers, creating upside optionality. The team refinanced the balance sheet, resolved legacy fiduciary litigation, and is progressing toward deal close. The narrative balances deal certainty against the potential for a bidding war, reflecting the long‑awaited sale following the post‑spin safe‑harbor period.
