Business & market dynamics: Stardust Power is advancing its planned large‑scale lithium refinery in Muskogee, Oklahoma, with site work now underway after independent reviews validated low technical risk and an air‑quality permit was secured. A U.S. Department of Energy partnership to extract lithium from waste streams adds federal alignment and feedstock diversification. However, the company remains pre‑revenue, and a going‑concern warning underscores acute cash strain, making the domestic supply‑chain narrative heavily contingent on securing fresh funding.

Management strategy and execution: The team has methodically delivered de‑risking milestones—FEL‑3 engineering, permitting, and an execution‑ready contractor—while also capturing a DOE research role, demonstrating operational follow‑through. Yet concurrent registration of a vast block of shares for resale and management’s admission that survival hinges on near‑term capital expose deep financial distress. This dynamic erodes confidence in capital strategy and signals a high risk of extreme dilution that could overwhelm the project’s long‑term promise.
