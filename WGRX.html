INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-01

Business & market dynamics: Wellgistics is transitioning from early-stage development to commercial scaling, with its pharmacy segment showing accelerating monthly momentum and new patient access via a telepharmacy joint venture. The company is positioning itself as a disruptive alternative to legacy pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) at a time when regulatory pressure is squeezing the opaque spread-pricing and rebate practices of incumbents. Its blockchain and smart-contract platform aims to monetize prescription flows transparently, while an expanding ecosystem—spanning an AI-powered dispensing hub, a forthcoming health-and-wellness app, and medical-drone initiatives—broadens its healthcare footprint.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is pursuing a vertically integrated, tech-enabled strategy anchored by the PharmacyChain™ platform and the pending DelivMeds AI transaction. Recent actions include a capital raise that extends runway and meets transaction-closing conditions, a debt restructuring, and a lock-up agreement that signals discipline around potential dilution. A shareholder letter articulates a clear vision of becoming essential healthcare infrastructure, and management is coupling cost optimization with targeted partnership and product launches to demonstrate execution credibility during a formative growth phase.
