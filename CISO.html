The company faces a tough broader market environment, with risk-off sentiment and S&P 500 weakness amplifying skepticism around small-cap, unprofitable names. A recent volume spike in the stock lacked any fundamental catalyst, leaving technicals weak and raising concerns that the software transition has yet to demonstrate durable commercial traction or a clear inflection point.

However, the absence of a visible catalyst during the stock’s volatile bounce suggests the market questions execution credibility, particularly given the company’s reliance on external funding. The pivot reads as a necessary but unproven gamble, with no concrete evidence yet that the new sales channel can overcome the legacy revenue decline and deliver self-sustaining growth.
