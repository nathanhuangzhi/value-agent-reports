Business & market dynamics: The Vafseo launch is gathering strong momentum, marked by rapid patient growth, record product-level demand, and expanding adoption of observed dosing protocols across dialysis organizations. This promising uptake in the anemia franchise is partly offset by a declining legacy portfolio: Auryxia faces intensifying generic competition and pricing pressure, which is eroding its contribution. Beyond current commercial products, an early-stage kidney-disease pipeline adds a longer-term growth dimension, with a Phase 2 praliciguat study in FSGS actively enrolling.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a focused strategy—driving Vafseo’s commercial ramp, building clinical evidence to position it as a standard of care, and advancing a renal-focused pipeline. The virtual R&D Day underscored a commitment to transparency and upcoming catalysts. Yet, despite the operational progress, the market’s initial negative reaction to the quarter signals that investors remain unconvinced the accelerating new product can fully absorb the legacy erosion without more clarity on the path forward.
