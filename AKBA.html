The virtual R&D Day underscored a commitment to transparency and upcoming catalysts. Yet, despite the operational progress, the market’s initial negative reaction to the quarter signals that investors remain unconvinced the accelerating new product can fully absorb the legacy erosion without more clarity on the path forward.
