The nascent pipeline in colorectal and breast cancer represents an optionality that the market may be undervaluing.

With a healthy cash balance and a reiterated full-year revenue outlook, management’s posture is one of controlled aggression — prioritizing market build-out over near-term margin maximization. The repeated guidance updates and transparent operational metrics reinforce a narrative of improving execution credibility after a period of share-price weakness.
