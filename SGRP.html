Business & market dynamics: SPAR Group is pivoting away from low-margin US Remodel projects toward higher-margin recurring merchandising services, which has pressured revenue but lifted gross margins and brought a return to positive EBITDA. The pipeline supports guided revenue growth, though prior period losses and negative cash flow have triggered Nasdaq deficiency notices, underscoring fragile financial footing.

Management strategy and execution: Management under CEO Linnane is executing the margin-focused shift and reaffirming guidance. Yet founder Robert G. Brown is agitating for a pivot to AI retail software, a multi-year buyback, a small dividend tied to free cash flow, and a ban on sub-$1 equity issuances. The activist push reveals internal discord on strategy and capital allocation, potentially undermining management’s credibility.
