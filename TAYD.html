They managed through government‑shutdown delays while still delivering record results, and they are proactively seeding demand in adjacent structural applications. The company maintains a fortress‑like balance sheet and a reputation for technical excellence—reinforced by a BAE Systems supplier award—which supports its credibility and selective approach to growth without overextending.
