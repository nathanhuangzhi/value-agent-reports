INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Modular Medical is a pre-revenue developer of a tubeless insulin patch pump, targeting the large “almost-pumper” segment of the diabetes market. The company has reached a pivotal regulatory milestone with a 510(k) submission for its Pivot pump, positioning it to enter a compelling market niche. However, the pre-commercial stage brings acute capital needs; the firm faces an existential cash crunch just as it attempts to cross the chasm from R&D to commercialization. No revenue base exists, and the narrative is dominated by the high stakes of securing a commercial launch before funding runs dry.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a classic pre-revenue transition: filing the key regulatory submission and simultaneously addressing the balance-sheet strain through a public offering. The raise directly funds the Pivot pump launch effort, demonstrating management’s ability to secure a financial lifeline at a critical juncture. Executing the 510(k) clearance and converting regulatory success into manufacturing readiness and market uptake will now test operational credibility. The strategy centers on bridging the gap to first revenue, with execution risk tightly linked to near-term capitalization and commercial ramp capabilities.
