INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-07

Business & market dynamics: Chegg’s legacy academic services continue to shrink under intense competitive pressure from free generative AI tools, while its newer skilling segment shows early, modest traction but lacks scale. The company is also pivoting into a B2B AI model training opportunity, which adds an asymmetric turnaround dimension. Overall, customer retention and product utility remain unproven, leaving the core business in a disrupted, deep-value state with highly uncertain demand patterns.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership exceeded expectations, returning the firm to profitability for the first time in two years and pointing to a foundation for future growth built around skilling and the AI training pivot. Despite a massive multiyear stock decline, a recent surge in call-option activity suggests some market participants see credibility in the restructuring. Management’s ability to sustain execution and convert nascent initiatives into durable revenue streams remains the central open question.
