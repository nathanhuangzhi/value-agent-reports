INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: InspireMD is building strong commercial momentum in the carotid stent market, transitioning from a pre-revenue stage to meaningful U.S. adoption of its CGuard Prime device. Early U.S. launch execution is yielding rapid case-volume growth and initial penetration into major integrated delivery networks. International demand remains durable across many markets, while the forthcoming approval of a longer CGuard Prime variant for transcarotid artery revascularization (TCAR) and progress on a neuroprotection system (SwitchGuard) are poised to meaningfully expand the addressable opportunity. Competitive positioning is strengthening as the device gains physician recognition.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a disciplined, catalyst-rich roadmap. The team successfully guided the company through FDA clearance, a commercial launch, and initial U.S. revenue generation, quickly establishing CGuard Prime as a go-to device among operators. Management is advancing a robust clinical pipeline—including IDE approval for the CGUARDIANS III pivotal trial—and has set clear near-term regulatory milestones that double the accessible market. Strategic capital allocation and operational focus signal a credible, execution-driven narrative, with organizational alignment around scaling commercial reach and deepening clinical validation.
