INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: ClearOne’s core operations have deteriorated to the point where the company is no longer pursuing a going-concern strategy. Instead of signaling any operational turnaround or demand recovery, the narrative is defined by a planned sale of substantially all assets. The business faces such severe and sustained pressure that management sees no viable path forward through organic growth, indicating that competitive, market, or structural headwinds have likely rendered the legacy audiovisual communications model non-viable.

Management strategy and execution: Management is executing a defensive and forced restructuring to avoid Nasdaq delisting. The capital raise—a convertible note with punitive conversion terms and mandatory board seats for the lender—shows limited negotiating leverage. A newly created redeemable preferred dividend must be funded from asset sale proceeds within a tight 180‑day window, with liquidation as the fallback. Board expansion and a reverse stock split further underscore that leadership is navigating a crisis, not a turnaround. Credibility is under extreme pressure as every move is about regulatory survival and orderly exit rather than value creation.
