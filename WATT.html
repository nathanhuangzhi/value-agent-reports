Business & market dynamics: The company is converting a once-speculative wireless power vision into tangible commercial traction, landing large-scale rollouts with Fortune 10 customers in logistics and e-commerce. A multi-vertical pipeline now extends into retail, grocery, manufacturing, and regulated sectors, fueled by demand for real-time asset tracking. Revenue has surged and sequential growth is holding, though the competitive environment remains intense and mixed analyst ratings suggest the market is not yet fully convinced of durable demand.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has moved from proofs-of-concept to deployments, expanded an international program with a key customer, and kept a tight grip on operating expenses, narrowing losses while maintaining a comfortable cash runway. The string of consecutive growth quarters signals improving execution, but the lingering consensus of hold or sell ratings from analysts indicates that credibility will require further evidence that these early wins translate into sustainable, profitable scale.
