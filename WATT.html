INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-17

Business & market dynamics: Energous is gaining commercial traction with its battery-free wireless power networks, as two Fortune 10 customers deploy the technology at scale—one targeting thousands of U.S. locations with over 1,500 already installed, and another expanding a multi-use-case international program. A growing proof-of-concept pipeline spans retail, logistics, grocery, quick-service restaurants, manufacturing, and government sectors, addressing a large asset-tracking and compliance need. The narrative is buoyant, with the company highlighting five consecutive quarters of revenue growth and strong momentum from its scalable, over-the-air power solutions.

Management strategy and execution: CEO Mallorie Burak described the recent quarter as a defining moment in the company’s evolution, emphasizing execution on large enterprise rollouts and new customer programs. The leadership is backing expansion with fresh funding and pointing to robust proofs of concept. However, market credibility is mixed: while one research firm upgraded to hold, another maintained a sell rating, suggesting that despite operational progress, the broader analyst community remains cautious about the outlook.
