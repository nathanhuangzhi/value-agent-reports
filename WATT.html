Business & market dynamics: Energous is gaining commercial traction as its battery-free wireless power network secures large-scale deployments with multiple Fortune 10 customers, spanning thousands of U.S. locations and expanding internationally. Use cases in asset tracking, e‑commerce, and logistics are driving a growing proof‑of‑concept pipeline across retail, grocery, manufacturing, and regulated sectors. This operational momentum has supported five consecutive quarters of revenue growth, signaling improving product‑market fit and accelerating enterprise adoption.

Management strategy and execution: Management is executing on high‑visibility rollouts, with one Fortune 10 program advancing past early installation milestones and a second global customer broadening its multi‑use‑case program. The company has secured new funding to sustain innovation and expansion. Despite this operational progress, analyst commentary remains divided and cautious — one Hold and one Sell rating are the only published views — suggesting some market participants question the path to scalable profitability even as real‑world adoption builds.
