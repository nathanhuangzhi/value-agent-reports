The overall demand picture is mixed — legacy operations face headwinds while the new product category signals encouraging end-market acceptance and expansion potential.

Management strategy and execution: The team executed a deliberate diversification move by acquiring the DCS loudspeaker business, and integration is progressing well with shipments scaling within the first two quarters. The shift toward proprietary, higher-margin products is improving the quality of revenue, and management is actively engaging the market through earnings calls, signaling transparency. This demonstrates a credible effort to reposition the company beyond its traditional cinema services, though the full payoff depends on sustained loudspeaker momentum and recovery in the core project pipeline.
