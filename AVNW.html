INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-19

Business & market dynamics: Aviat’s integration of Pasolink and IRU600 into an all-indoor microwave platform is unlocking a significant new addressable market in North American capacity upgrades and international long-haul backbone networks. Demand is supported by 5G- and IoT-driven wireless backhaul needs. A key narrative risk is the long-term threat that fiber displacement could erode microwave backhaul’s relevance.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has moved quickly to merge the Pasolink acquisition, launch the Pasolink LH long-haul product, and extend into Thailand, signaling a deliberate push toward higher-growth international markets. Profitability has been achieved, and the market is responding to credible execution, with an innovation roadmap that appears to be delivering on its promise.
