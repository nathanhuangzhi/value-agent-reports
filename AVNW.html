INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-19

Business & market dynamics: The company’s all-indoor microwave platform expansion, merging Pasolink and IRU600, has unlocked a significant market opportunity, reducing customer operational costs while strengthening North American and international trunking capabilities. Demand is fueled by private networks, 5G deployments, and rural broadband across geographies including Thailand. The business achieved profitability, and near-term momentum is strong, though the longer-term narrative acknowledges potential headwinds from fiber-based backhaul competition and periodic stock volatility.

Management strategy and execution: Management has effectively integrated the Pasolink acquisition and broadened the product line with the MB Max offering and a new long-haul solution, backed by the targeted all-indoor platform strategy. Improved margins and a robust forward outlook reflect solid execution and growing credibility. Capital allocation focuses on complementary acquisitions and internal R&D that align with secular wireless transport demand, positioning the company to capture the current investment cycle.
