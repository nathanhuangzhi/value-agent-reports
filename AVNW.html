INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-19

Business & market dynamics: Aviat’s microwave networking business is showing positive momentum, centered on an expanded all-indoor platform that integrates the Pasolink acquisition. This launch targets greater capacity and reliability for North American customers while adding a new long-haul solution for international trunking and backbone markets. Demand is supported by 5G and IoT-driven backhaul needs, though the segment faces an ongoing competitive headwind from fiber alternatives. Geographic exposure remains diversified across multiple continents, and the product refresh is unlocking a significant, previously untapped market layer.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is successfully executing on its acquisition strategy, rapidly integrating Pasolink into a unified product portfolio and following through with new offerings that address tangible customer pain points—such as reducing tower climbs and operational costs via the all-indoor design. The move to profitability and the pursuit of a large incremental market opportunity signal improving execution credibility. Early expansion signals into Thailand and the focus on long-haul trunking suggest management is deliberately broadening the company’s addressable environment beyond its traditional base.
