INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: The core CytoSorb business is growing modestly with improving gross margins, anchored by a high-margin consumable model and broad distribution across more than 70 countries. The dominant investment narrative, however, revolves around the U.S. regulatory pathway for DrugSorb‑ATR, a blood purification therapy for cardiac surgery. The pivotal STAR‑T study has been accepted for publication in a top journal, lending scientific credibility, while a De Novo pre-submission meeting with the FDA is imminent. A potential U.S. launch represents a transformational market expansion, making the regulatory decision the single most consequential catalyst.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is demonstrating disciplined execution by implementing a strategic workforce and cost-reduction program to accelerate the timeline to cash flow breakeven, supported by an amended credit facility to strengthen the balance sheet. Concurrently, they are actively advancing the DrugSorb‑ATR De Novo application through interactive FDA dialogue and maintaining investor engagement. The proactive cost management alongside the regulatory push reflects a pragmatic strategy to self‑fund the pathway to a key value‑inflection point, a shift that has been noted favorably by the analyst community.
