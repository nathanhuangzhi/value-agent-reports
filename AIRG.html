The business is shifting toward system‑level connectivity solutions, with enterprise and automotive providing sequential lift that offsets consumer softness. Management strategy and execution: Management is driving a deliberate transformation by converting last year’s groundwork into commercial design wins while maintaining cost discipline. The tone is one of steady execution—operating expenses are being managed lower year over year, and guidance points to a stronger second quarter. Analysts have responded by raising price targets, reflecting growing confidence in margin execution and the earnings visibility from new wins. The leadership team, led by a relatively new CEO, appears to be rebuilding credibility by aligning strategic priorities with operational progress, even as profitability remains a work in progress.
