Business & market dynamics: Airgain’s pivot to system‑level connectivity is gaining traction. Enterprise and automotive revenue grew sequentially, driven by embedded modem and AirgainConnect gateway demand, while consumer sales experienced normal seasonal softness. Recent design wins—a multi‑year 5G home platform with a Tier‑1 North American operator, a sizeable follow‑on IoT order, and a robotics design‑in—highlight broadening adoption and a diversifying revenue base beyond legacy components.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is converting prior strategic groundwork into commercial momentum. Tight cost discipline lowered operating expenses year‑over‑year, and Q1 results landed at guidance with a slightly improved adjusted EBITDA loss. A stronger Q2 outlook signals that accelerating design‑win conversions and enterprise/automotive growth will increasingly counter consumer headwinds, reinforcing market confidence in the transformation.
