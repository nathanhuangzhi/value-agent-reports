The narrative has shifted from volatility-driven caution to a focus on durable margin expansion and capacity-driven growth. However, some market commentary raises valuation concerns, noting the stock may be overvalued relative to fundamentals even after the strong results, which injects a note of caution into the otherwise bullish demand story.

By delivering consecutive profitable quarters and challenging prior volatility-focused narratives, management is rebuilding credibility and suggesting that the recent profit inflection could be sustained through operational leverage rather than one-time tailwinds.
