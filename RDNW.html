INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-23

Business & market dynamics: RideNow is riding a wave of secular powersports demand, with robust same-store unit volumes driving the top line. The industry’s projected expansion provides a structural tailwind, but the narrative is tempered by a declining gross profit per unit — suggesting mix shifts or price pressure — and a notable cash outflow from operations. Inventory also rose, raising questions about working capital management even as sales remain strong. While the underlying consumer appetite appears healthy, the disconnect between revenue momentum and free cash flow generation leaves the outlook mixed.

Management strategy and execution: Management is delivering on operating leverage, tightening SG&A and improving adjusted profitability, which has allowed some de-leveraging alongside store expansion. Analysts have raised price targets in recognition of same-store strength, yet ratings remain neutral, signaling persistent caution around liquidity, negative cash flow, and the sustainability of margin gains. The market appears to be in a show-me state: acknowledging the operational turnaround but withholding full confidence until cash conversion and balance-sheet resilience become more tangible.
