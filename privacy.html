Valueland Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Valueland is a value-investing research app. This page describes what data the app handles and what it does not.

Information we collect

None. Valueland does not require an account, does not ask for personal information, and does not track usage. The app makes anonymous HTTPS requests to its public data CDN (value-agent-reports.vercel.app) to fetch the daily research payloads. No identifiers about you or your device are sent beyond what your network stack adds by default (e.g. IP address visible to the CDN, which is not stored).

Where the data comes from

  • SEC EDGAR — annual and quarterly financial statements (10-K / 10-Q XBRL filings), public domain.
  • Yahoo Finance (yfinance) — price history and supplementary financial statement data.
  • Exa — public web articles cited as sources in the narrative.
  • DeepSeek — large language model that writes the per-ticker Munger-style analysis narrative.

All four are queried from our backend pipeline, not from the app itself. The app only reads the resulting static JSON files.

Third-party services

The app contacts only the Valueland data CDN. It does not embed analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDK that would communicate with external servers.

Not investment advice

The information in Valueland is provided for research and educational purposes only. It is not a recommendation, solicitation, or endorsement to buy, sell, or hold any security. Consult a qualified financial professional before making investment decisions. We make no guarantee about the accuracy or timeliness of the data shown.

Children

Valueland is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be highlighted in a future app release.
