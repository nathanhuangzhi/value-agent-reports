INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-12

Business & market dynamics: Certara’s core software business is showing clear momentum, with strong bookings growth and healthy uptake of Simcyp, Phoenix, and Chemaxon platforms indicating robust demand from biopharma for model-informed drug development tools. Services remained soft, particularly in the regulatory writing and medical writing segment; the completed divestiture of that unit removes a drag and tilts the revenue mix decisively toward higher-quality recurring software streams.

Management strategy and execution: New CEO Jon Resnick moved swiftly to sharpen focus by selling the non-core regulatory and medical writing business, updating guidance to reflect a streamlined, software-centric model. The significant gap between software bookings growth and reported revenue points to successful go-to-market execution and a strengthening forward pipeline, lending early credibility to the strategic pivot.
