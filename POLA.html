Profitability has turned the corner, though a one-time warranty reserve benefit flattered the recent result. These actions have shored up working capital and equity, but the company’s credibility hinges on sustaining operational improvements without relying on further one-offs. Execution appears focused on converting the growing backlog and carefully managing cash to navigate the tight liquidity position.
