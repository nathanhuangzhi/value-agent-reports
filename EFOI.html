INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-03

Business & market dynamics: Demand is recovering across both commercial and military maritime lighting segments, with the latter showing particular strength. The company is simultaneously moving beyond its legacy LED business into energy infrastructure, highlighted by a joint investment in a Japanese energy storage plant. This project has already secured a grid application response from Kyushu Electric Power, with commercial operation targeted for the second half of 2026, offering entry into high-value power regulation services.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is pursuing a deliberate pivot into energy storage, taking the lead on battery procurement and energy management for the Asakura project. This move diversifies the business from a niche lighting provider toward an energy infrastructure player. A modest private placement of shares to the Japanese partner, Euka Power, signals alignment and provides funding, underscoring management’s commitment to executing the international expansion.
