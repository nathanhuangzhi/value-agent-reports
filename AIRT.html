INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-29

Business & market dynamics: Air T is experiencing broad-based momentum across its diversified aviation and industrial segments, with organic expansion supplemented by recent acquisitions. The overnight cargo, ground support, and regional airline operations are riding steady demand, while the company’s push into aviation asset management is markedly gaining scale. The closure of Crestone Air Partners’ acquisition of Arena Aviation Capital vaults assets under management to a multiple of prior levels, signaling strong appetite for aircraft leasing and fee-based services. This expansion reflects favorable end-market conditions in global aviation, where airlines and lessors seek capital and fleet solutions. The integrated portfolio model appears to be capturing demand across both operating businesses and asset-light management platforms.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is actively executing a buy-to-build permanent-capital strategy, using the parent company’s resources to incubate and scale independent, yet interrelated, businesses. The Crestone-Arena transaction demonstrates disciplined deal-making and a clear blueprint for bolt-on growth, with the simultaneous equity investment from Blue Owl Capital providing external validation of the asset manager’s value. Management’s decision to retain majority ownership while welcoming a strategic minority partner reflects a balanced approach to funding and alignment. The accretion of assets under management and the expansion of fee streams suggest that the team is effectively converting acquisitions into platform value, bolstering its credibility with the market. Overall, the narrative points to confident capital allocation and an organizational structure designed to compound intrinsic value over time.
