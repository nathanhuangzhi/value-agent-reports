INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-03

Business & market dynamics: The core business is under clear pressure, with comparable sales declining and store performance weakening more than the direct channel. A cautious consumer environment and a shifting customer base are weighing on demand. While management points to improving conversion rates and average order values as bright spots, the headline trend reflects a retreat from prior-year levels. The proposed merger with FullBeauty Brands has been thrown into doubt, adding strategic overhang and uncertainty about the brand’s future path in a niche retail segment.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is emphasizing technological and product-focused pivots—notably FitMap and a push into private brands—to rekindle growth. However, execution is taking place against a backdrop of widening losses and a materially leaner cash position, which constrains room for missteps. The merger process that once promised scale is now flagged as uncertain, testing management’s credibility. The narrative is therefore one of a leadership team simultaneously defending against near-term headwinds while attempting to rewire the business’s long-term positioning, leaving the market guarded about execution follow-through.
