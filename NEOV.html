The CFO and EVP recently purchased shares on the open market, aligning insider interests with shareholders. Analysts applaud the strategic milestones, including the Georgia factory timeline and the conversion of a sizable project pipeline into a purchase order. The narrative reflects a management team that has weathered dilution pain and is now delivering on its platform vision, restoring credibility as commercial operations begin to scale.
