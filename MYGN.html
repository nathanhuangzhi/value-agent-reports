INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-27

Business & market dynamics: Myriad is pivoting toward its Cancer Care Continuum, where hereditary cancer testing volumes are rising robustly, supported by newly launched products such as its Precise MRD assay and FDA approval for a companion diagnostic. This oncology momentum is partly offset by a shrinking Prenatal Health franchise, leaving total testing volumes roughly flat. The demand environment for cancer genetics appears healthy, while the prenatal headwind reflects a product-line decline rather than macro weakness, keeping the revenue mix in transition.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is leaning heavily into the cancer pivot, ramping commercial spend to support multiple oncology launches, which pressured near‑term profitability and contributed to a first‑quarter earnings miss. Management reiterated full‑year guidance, signaling confidence that investments will yield a second‑half inflection, but the earnings shortfall and cost ramp have left the market in a “show‑me” stance. The delivery of regulatory milestones demonstrates operational follow‑through, yet credibility now hinges on converting elevated spending into sustained growth without further margin erosion.
