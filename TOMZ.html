Concurrently, the planned Carbonium Core merger introduces a distinct, high-upside pivot into nuclear-grade graphite, targeting the critical-materials supply chain for an AI-driven nuclear renaissance as a domestic-first imperative.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is balancing organic growth discipline — evidenced by cost controls and a growing integration pipeline — with a bold, transformative merger strategy. The non-binding letter of intent for Carbonium Core outlines a phased roadmap to build what management envisions as the first commercial-scale U.S. nuclear graphite facility. Credibility now depends on converting the LOI into definitive agreements and successfully navigating the complex path from pilot to full-scale production.
