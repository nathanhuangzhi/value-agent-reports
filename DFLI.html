INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-15

Business & market dynamics: The RV direct-to-consumer channel is experiencing soft demand, but a large purchase order from Stevens Transport covering nearly 500 trucks signals a meaningful push into commercial vehicle electrification. Recent European and Japanese patent allowances for dry electrode technology strengthen the IP moat and could unlock future licensing avenues. Still, near-term revenue remains tied to legacy products, and the weak balance sheet dominates market attention, with some viewing the patent progress as a distraction from underlying financial fragility.

Management strategy and execution: Management exceeded Q1 guidance and is on track with cost reductions intended to narrow losses sequentially. The Stevens Transport win demonstrates credible execution in diversifying beyond cyclical RV markets. However, persistently poor financial health ratings and a heavy debt load erode confidence, prompting some to dismiss the patent news as a sideshow. While the improved Q2 guidance hints at operational progress, leadership credibility is constrained by the urgent need to address liquidity and balance-sheet risk.
