Business & market dynamics: The core Advanced Wound Care franchise has been hit by a sharp, unexpected reversal following CMS reimbursement policy changes and market confusion that severely disrupted demand. While the smaller Surgical & Sports Medicine segment held steady, the wound care business—the company’s primary revenue driver—experienced a dramatic contraction, eroding gross margins and highlighting deep vulnerability to regulatory shifts. The prior period of growth and bullish momentum vanished overnight, leaving the business suddenly exposed to a hostile reimbursement environment.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is now grappling with a credibility crisis after a stark divergence between recent bullish analyst upgrades and the Q1 2026 collapse. The sudden disclosure of coverage-driven headwinds, inventory write-downs, and operational disarray suggests either poor visibility into policy risk or a failure to prepare. Management’s commentary points to efforts to navigate the confusion and stabilize the wound care platform, but the abrupt guidance miss and the collapse in profitability have shaken market trust, raising serious questions about execution and strategic foresight.
