The CEO has voiced cautious optimism about a recovery, citing order growth trends, yet the market has reacted negatively to earnings misses and an analyst downgrade to “sell.” The narrative reflects a management team focused on discipline and shareholder returns, though credibility is being tested by persistent quarterly underperformance and uncertain demand visibility.
