A well-attended process burner demonstration at Zeeco showcased the new Gen 2 technology, signaling growing industry validation. The increasing demand for emissions reduction and hydrogen-ready combustion solutions provides a supportive regulatory backdrop, positioning the advanced burner portfolio favorably in North American industrial energy markets.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is coupling field-level execution with proactive capital activity to sustain growth. The Company revived an at-the-market equity program and concluded a firm-commitment underwritten public offering—including over-allotment—to reinforce its balance sheet without specifying a one-time funding event. Operationally, management is deepening customer relationships, converting initial orders into full-system scopes, and leveraging strategic demonstrations with established partners such as Zeeco. CEO Jim Deller’s commentary underscores a deliberate strategy of technology validation and repeat business development, though the equity dilution path signals a near-term need for external funding to fully capitalize on emerging demand.
