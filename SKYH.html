INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-06-07

Business & market dynamics: The narrative centers on a structural imbalance: record business jet deliveries are colliding with a chronic shortage of Class A hangar space at America’s busiest airports. Sky Harbour is the only public pure-play building a national network of premium private-aviation campuses on irreplaceable, supply-constrained airport land. The demand backdrop is exceptionally supportive, driven by expanding large-cabin fleets and the enduring appeal of private aviation, positioning the company’s Home Base Operator model directly at the intersection of aviation growth and real-estate scarcity.

Management strategy and execution: Management is executing a capital-intensive, asset-heavy buildout of hangar campuses under the HALO (Heavy Assets, Low Obsolescence) philosophy, targeting long-term ground leases at premier airports. The strategic posture is one of aggressive expansion: capital has been secured, early campuses are showing operational traction, and the business is approaching cash-flow breakeven. However, execution remains a mixed story—strong revenue momentum and construction progress coexist with a profile of negative EBITDA and high leverage, leaving the market split between believers in a durable infrastructure moat and skeptics waiting for proof of sustainable cash generation.
