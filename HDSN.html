Business & market dynamics: Hudson’s reclamation model is leveraged to the HFC phasedown and the 2029 supply crunch narrative. After losing a major DLA contract, operations stabilized through a bridge extension running to mid-2027. The refrigerant lineup expanded via a licensing deal and a CARB pilot, diversifying end‑market exposure, though profitability remained under pressure even as revenue grew.

Management strategy and execution: Management’s swift bridge negotiation after the contract loss and the licensing deal for next‑gen refrigerants signal a pragmatic execution pivot. Major shareholder Hartree Partners followed these moves with aggressive open‑market buying, lifting its stake markedly, which underscores confidence in the refocused strategy and the long‑term reclamation opportunity.
