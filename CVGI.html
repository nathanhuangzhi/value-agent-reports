Business & market dynamics: The company is pivoting from cyclical Class 8 truck exposure toward higher-margin electrical systems, exemplified by the Zoox robotaxi contract, which could unlock margin expansion and decouple revenues from trucking cycles. Core operations are stabilizing, with recent product mix and operational gains supporting expectations of a market recovery. However, significant headwinds persist, including a looming 2027 EPA regulatory cliff, weak European EV demand, and geopolitical-driven supply-chain disruptions that have pressured industrial costs—though a potential Iran peace signal recently eased some cyclical fears.

Management strategy and execution: Activist investor Lakeview’s board presence is driving a strategy focused on ROIC optimization, asset divestitures, and margin extraction, signaling deliberate capital allocation. Management reaffirmed full-year guidance and used a sale-leaseback to accelerate leverage reduction, addressing balance-sheet concerns. Yet execution credibility is tempered by high net leverage, recent CFO turnover, and the inherent risk in transforming the business mix while navigating a binary operating environment.
