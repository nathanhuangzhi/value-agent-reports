INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-01

Business & market dynamics: GSIT is a two-speed story: a legacy SRAM business enjoying strong order flow from chip design and simulation customers, lifting sales and product mix, while a speculative edge-AI pivot around the Gemini-II APU gathers early, high-risk momentum. The AI side is still pre-revenue, but recent validation includes a defense drone proof-of-concept demo and a Phase 1 smart-city project award. Market sentiment is split—bearish sell-side calls contrast with a technical breakout above the 200‑day moving average, and heavy insider selling adds a cautionary note.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is running a self-funded model, using stable SRAM cash flows to advance the APU roadmap without external capital pressure. Execution met key defense software milestones on schedule, enabling the drone demonstration, and quickly converted the post-quarter smart-city win. Yet credibility is tested by significant insider share disposals and a lack of analyst support, clouding the market’s view of whether management will translate early AI contracts into a durable commercial business.
