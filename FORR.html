INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-15

Business & market dynamics: The core research franchise faces structural pressure as enterprise clients explore in-house analytics using large language models, contributing to declining contract value and client counts. AI-driven competition threatens traditional syndicated research demand. Offsetting this, Forrester’s own surveys point to rebounding tech budgets in 2027, and early AI-enhanced platform features—such as integrations with Microsoft Teams—are drawing increased client engagement and modestly improving retention. The product portfolio is expanding to address evolving enterprise needs, though meaningful AI monetization remains elusive.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is pivoting the business toward AI-augmented products and embedding its research within collaboration tools, signaling an adaptive response to the competitive landscape. The strategy has yet to translate into positive operating momentum, with sell-side analysts assigning Sell or Hold ratings and expressing low confidence in near-term visibility. Despite a strong balance sheet, the early-stage AI pivot has not arrested the ongoing earnings contraction, and the recent share-price bounce does not appear driven by fundamental improvement, leaving the market to question execution credibility.
