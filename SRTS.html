Business & market dynamics: Sensus is in a transition period following the January 2026 introduction of dedicated CPT codes for superficial radiotherapy. Headline comparisons were distorted by the absence of a historically dominant customer, but underlying demand from a wider customer base grew, pointing to a more diversified and sustainable sales pattern. Early billing feedback is positive, with providers successfully billing CMS under the new codes, a signal that could accelerate broader adoption of the company’s SRT systems.

Management strategy and execution: Leaders describe the new reimbursement environment as a fundamental shift and have dedicated the quarter to educating customers and training them on the new coding structure. Chairman and CEO Joe Sardano cited “excellent” initial results, reflecting confidence that proactive guidance will build recurring treatment volumes. The strategy focuses on broadening the user base and embedding SRT as a standard-of-care, reducing reliance on lumpy one-time sales while positioning the company to capture regulatory tailwinds.
