INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: The first quarter marked a pivotal transition as dedicated CPT reimbursement codes for superficial radiotherapy took effect, shifting the commercial landscape. While total revenue contracted sharply due to the absence of sales to the historically largest customer, underlying growth from a broader customer mix accelerated. Early physician feedback on the new coding structure is positive, pointing to a potential broadening of end-market demand. Treatment volumes and recurring revenue streams are expanding, and the company is seeing healthy demand for both direct system sales and flexible placement arrangements.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership describes the new code environment as “fundamentally different” and committed much of the quarter to educating customers and training them on billing processes. The tone on the earnings call was upbeat, with management highlighting “excellent” initial reimbursement results. Strategic efforts are focused on diversifying the customer base away from historical concentration and deepening recurring revenue through rental and Fair Deal Agreement placements, which signals a deliberate shift toward more predictable, service-oriented revenue.
