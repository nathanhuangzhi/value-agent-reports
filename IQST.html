The pending majority‑stake acquisition of Ultranet Telecom Group represents the largest transaction in the company’s history and is set to accelerate this shift, adding scale and a structural profitability uplift. Market sentiment has swung meaningfully bullish, reflected in a steep, 43% collapse in short interest over the past month as bearish bets unwind. The company’s underlying commercial momentum is robust, with strong organic top‑line expansion reinforcing its transition toward scalable, recurring digital revenue streams.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is orchestrating an aggressive transformation through M&A and direct capital‑return signals. The binding MOU for Ultranet demonstrates a clear commitment to jump‑starting profitability, while the simultaneous authorization of a share buyback underscores management’s view that the stock is undervalued. Funding the buyback in part with subsidiary dividends points to disciplined capital recycling. The board also reaffirmed ambitious full‑year revenue targets, lending credibility to the growth narrative. Investors have responded strongly, sending shares sharply higher on the deal announcement, and the dramatic retreat of short sellers indicates the market is beginning to accept management’s repositioning of iQSTEL as a digital‑services platform rather than a low‑multiple telecom carrier.
