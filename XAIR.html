INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Beyond Air’s commercial LungFit PH nitric oxide platform is gaining clear momentum, reflected in accelerating adoption and a shift to positive gross profit. The company is successfully building a revenue base in the hospital setting. Pipeline progress adds another dimension: the planned presentation of Phase 1a solid-tumor data from the UNO program at AACR in April 2026 positions oncology as a potentially significant future catalyst. Meanwhile, the monetization of the NeuroNOS subsidiary via a binding letter of intent with XTL Biopharmaceuticals highlights the unrealized value of non-core assets and brings a partner to advance that program.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a focused turnaround, marked by tight cost discipline that has dramatically lowered operating expenses while maintaining the full-year revenue outlook. The team has strengthened the balance sheet through a recent PIPE transaction, extending cash runway well into calendar 2027, and simultaneously structured the NeuroNOS deal to provide upfront cash, milestone potential, and retained equity upside without diluting core shareholders. This dual-track approach—driving commercial growth while unlocking asset value—reflects a pragmatic capital-allocation strategy that is stabilizing the enterprise and positioning it for multiple value-creating milestones.
