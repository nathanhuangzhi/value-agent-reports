However, the narrative is mixed: while the milestone and product portfolio growth suggest momentum in medical biomaterials, a sell rating from Weiss and a reduced price target from Ascendiant point to lingering uncertainty about adoption pace and the economics of scale‑up.

Actions include cost reductions, IP expansion, and strengthened liquidity, alongside the delivery of clinical and regulatory milestones. Management’s communications project confidence, yet the external analyst response reveals a “show‑me” posture — with a buy rating on nascent progress but tempered by a cut target and a contrasting sell call, underscoring that execution credibility remains to be fully earned.
