INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-02

Business & market dynamics: Anterix is shifting from pure long-term leasing toward a broader mix of spectrum sales and services, powered by the FCC’s expansion of its 900 MHz broadband segment. Utility demand for private networks stays strong, with new sale agreements adding CPS Energy, Texas-New Mexico Power, NorthWestern Energy, and Benton PUD to the roster, creating visible contracted business. The company’s essentially exclusive spectrum position and the favorable regulatory tailwind reinforce its scarcity value and market momentum.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is executing a deliberate pivot to a more varied revenue model, landing spectrum sale deals and locking in regulatory wins. The debt-free posture signals disciplined capital allocation. The strategic evolution is widely seen as sound, though the stock’s steep run has pushed some analysts to hold or neutral on valuation worries, while others corrected earlier rating mistakes and remain buy‑rated. Overall execution credibility is intact, with tangible contract wins backing the narrative.
