INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-08

Business & market dynamics: Alico’s core citrus operations are experiencing improved momentum, with higher orange production and better pricing driving the year-over-year gains. Beyond agriculture, the company is actively monetizing its land holdings, closing significant land sales and advancing the Corkscrew Grove Villages project through local entitlement approvals, with federal and state permits progressing. This dual revenue stream—agricultural output and real estate development—provides distinct, uncorrelated growth levers.

Management strategy and execution: Management is demonstrating disciplined capital allocation by using proceeds from land sales to aggressively repurchase shares and build a cash cushion that extends the operating runway. The team’s cost management is evident in lower operating expenses and stronger cash flow, while the steady progress on regulatory entitlements for its development project shows execution capability. The overall tone of the recent update was confident, signaling that management is delivering on both operational efficiency and long-term asset value realization without near-term liquidity concerns.
