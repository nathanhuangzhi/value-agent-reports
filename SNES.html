INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-02

Business & market dynamics: The hantavirus outbreak has spotlighted the public-health risks of rodent infestations, directly reinforcing the need for SenesTech’s fertility-control solutions. E-commerce now dominates sales, shifting the business toward a higher-margin direct model. Early traction in municipal and agricultural markets, via city deployments and international expansion, provides additional growth avenues. Yet the overall pace of commercial scaling remains measured, with several levers—large retail, subscriptions—still converting slowly.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership has been revamped to accelerate execution, with Michael Edell named CEO after overseeing operations and Amazon sales. The board was restructured to sharpen its commercial focus. Notably, large shareholder Glenbrook Capital Management raised its stake, signaling confidence in the new direction. The market’s attention is now on whether the refreshed leadership can translate the tailwinds into faster, sustained growth.
