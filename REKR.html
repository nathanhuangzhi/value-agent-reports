While still early‑stage, the combination of growing recurring revenue and a leaner cost base points to improving business quality.

Management described the current structure as “leaner, faster, and more focused” than a year ago, signaling a disciplined pivot from growth at any cost toward sustainable profitability. The credible progress toward positive EBITDA and the achievement of the company’s first cash‑generating quarter mark an important step in building market confidence that the turnaround strategy is taking hold.
