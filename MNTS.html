INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-05-14

Business & market dynamics: Momentus is transitioning from a pre-revenue startup to a contract-backed space services provider, highlighted by the successful Vigoride 7 mission with multiple payloads. New NASA and defense contracts are driving revenue from near-zero to meaningful levels, though the company remains deeply unprofitable. Demand signals are positive in government markets, but commercial adoption is still embryonic.

Management strategy and execution: Management has fortified the balance sheet by eliminating all debt and raising capital, removing near-term survival risk. CEO John Rood’s shareholder letter emphasizes disciplined execution, customer delivery, and a sharp revenue ramp guided by existing contracts. The pivot toward funded government work signals a more focused and credible strategy after years of cash burn.
