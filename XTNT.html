INVESTMENT NARRATIVE

Business & market dynamics: Xtant is pivoting toward a pure-play biologics focus after divesting its Coflex® assets and overseas hardware business. The core orthobiologics portfolio is showing healthy demand, underpinning sequential growth despite the removal of those non-core revenue streams. The divestitures compress the reported top line, but the underlying franchise is gaining traction, supported by procedural volume trends and a leaner product mix. The transition insulates the business from the hardware segment’s competitive and regulatory complexities, leaving a more concentrated, higher-margin wound-care and spinal biologics platform with a clearer growth path.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership executed a deliberate portfolio pruning, monetizing non-core assets to de-lever and sharpen strategic focus. The transactions unlocked cash proceeds that strengthened the balance sheet, while operational discipline converted this streamlined profile into positive net income, adjusted EBITDA, and operating cash flow. Management’s forward outlook resets expectations to a biologics-only base, forecasting sustained free cash flow generation and sequential core expansion. The narrative signals a maturing execution engine: realistic guidance, demonstrated expense control, and a pivot from acquisition-led growth to profitable organic development, which enhances credibility as a disciplined capital allocator.
