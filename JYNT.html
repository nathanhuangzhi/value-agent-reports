Business & market dynamics: System-wide sales and comparable clinic sales are declining as the clinic base contracts from closures of underperforming locations. Early improvements in member retention and patient metrics hint at underlying stabilization. The ongoing transition to a pure franchisor model means corporate revenue is increasingly driven by royalties and fees, while broader demand for chiropractic services carries some long-term uncertainty.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership is aggressively refranchising company-owned clinics and pruning weak sites to build an asset-light, higher-margin business. The strategy is already strengthening corporate profitability, reinforced by share buybacks and reaffirmed full-year guidance. Analyst sentiment remains mixed but includes recent upgrades to strong-buy, reflecting cautious optimism that the margin-focused turnaround can overcome soft system-wide top-line traction.
