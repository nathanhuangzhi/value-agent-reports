INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-02

Business & market dynamics: Peraso’s core fixed wireless access business faces irregular order patterns and lumpy demand, bruised by a recent customer order delay tied to supplier material shortages. Yet a sharp narrative shift is underway: the company scored a high-profile win in defense, supplying 60 GHz millimeter-wave technology for Israeli military drone identification-friend-or-foe systems, which stirred enthusiasm around electronic warfare and secure communications. Promotional commentary touts overlooked exposure to AI infrastructure, autonomous systems, and military drones, but these end markets remain nascent, and new customer orders have yet to establish predictable cadences. The business is thus a tale of two trajectories—legacy volatility versus early-stage defense momentum.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership acknowledged the supply hiccup and has since shipped the delayed order while implementing measures to reduce single-supplier reliance. Strategically, the team is steering capital toward drone, defense, and tactical communications products, crystallized by the Israeli contract and a new committed equity facility designed to fund working capital and product development. The facility offers liquidity but raises dilution concerns, underscoring a fragile balance between preserving runway and shareholder interests. The defense win boosted near-term credibility, yet the overall execution story remains mixed until erratic demand stabilizes and the pivot translates into sustained revenue momentum.
