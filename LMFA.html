Most recent source: 2026-05-16

Business & market dynamics: LM Funding achieved record energized hashrate and monthly Bitcoin production, reflecting expanding mining scale. However, weaker Bitcoin spot prices weighed on realized revenue from higher output. The company partially offset mining headwinds through growing energy curtailment sales, expected to increase seasonally at its Oklahoma site. Operations remain concentrated at U.S. facilities in Oklahoma and Mississippi.

Management strategy and execution: Management is pursuing a dual mining and Bitcoin treasury model. A significant held Bitcoin position exposed results to markdown-related losses this quarter. The team balanced mined-coin sales with HODL to fund operations while keeping the fleet stable after prior expansion. Leadership is actively diversifying into grid-service and curtailment revenue, adapting to volatile crypto market conditions.
