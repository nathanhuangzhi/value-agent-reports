INVESTMENT NARRATIVE
Most recent source: 2026-07-19

Business & market dynamics: Sky Quarry’s narrative hinges on the restart of Nevada’s only operating refinery amid tightening regional fuel supplies and elevated oil prices driven by geopolitical instability. The company aims to serve a market heavily dependent on imported fuel and has outlined a longer-term vision to produce low-carbon and sustainable aviation fuels. Yet operations remain pre-revenue, and the financial footing is precarious, typical of a speculative micro-cap turn-around in a capital-intensive industry.

Management strategy and execution: Leadership installed a veteran refiner to shift the Foreland plant from repairs into active production and promoted a vertically integrated concept linking its oil sands resource to the refinery. Early-stage, non‑binding MOUs for greener fuels signal ambition but carry little weight. The stock’s surge has been momentum-driven, with trading volume far exceeding market capitalization. A reverse-split history and acute balance-sheet fragility underscore serious execution risk and a high likelihood of dilutive financing.
